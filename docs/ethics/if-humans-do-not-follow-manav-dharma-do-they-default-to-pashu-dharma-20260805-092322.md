**Original Question:**  
If humans do not follow manav dharma, do they default to pashu dharma?

**Full Answer:**  
 Pashu dharma. When you say that if a human being does not follow manav dharma then they will default to following pashu dharma, fulfillment of sleep, hunger, freedom from fear, and reproduction-- it is important to say something more in this regard, otherwise the true picture is not presented. If people do not follow manav dharma i.e. do not do sadhana and strive to realize the infinite within, and lead a live which supports this effort, then they will "default to a life of pashu dharma". It is important to explain that human beings cannot follow pashu dharma; they are incapable of following pashu dharma. Rather, they abuse pashu dharma. Due to their having an infinite desire, they abuse the faculty of pashu dharma within them, at every turn exceeding what is needed. Animals living naturally in the wild are beautiful to watch. They follow their dharma perfectly. It is quite elegant, seeing antelope grazing in the fields, or elephants moving in their herd. And they never overeat, never get overweight or cause chronic disease, never over accumulate things.  Whereas, human beings overeat and over-consume in every aspect of animal dharma, thereby degenerating themselves to a status beneath animals and become stones in their next life. So simply saying that if humans do not follow manava dharma they will follow pashu dharma is not an accurate statement; what they will do is to abuse pashu dharma.
