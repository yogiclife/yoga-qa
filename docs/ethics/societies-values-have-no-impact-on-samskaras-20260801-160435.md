Namaskar X ji,

Very nice session last week! 

Just want to ensure the point about societal values is clear-- 

- Society's values have zero impact on determining karmaphala and whether mind goes up or down. It is not that societal values also have some impact. The only factor which has any impact, and it is the all-determining factor, is whether one's actions are helping or harming others. That is universal.

- Society is made up of foolish people, hence the values and laws are full of flaws. The goal is for values and laws of society to come to be in full sync with punya and papa. That is, we will say that society has fully matured when all that is papa is against the law (such as eating meat, etc), and all that is punya is extolled by society (such as fighting against exploitation and injustice, etc). There is a gap due to society's foolishness, as a result of which society often condones and supports things which are bad. And that is also what generates the differences in values between one society and another: The type or variety of foolishness may vary from one society to another. Whereas punya (paropakAra) and papa (parApakAra) do not vary based on time-place-person; these are universal and these are all that matter.

- See all the examples you gave: slavery in US, atankavada in Islam, widow self-immolation in Hinduism, human sacrifice in South America, jativada in Japan. All these prove that society is foolish and thus society's values have no impact whatsoever on determining karmaphala and whether a mind will go up or down.
