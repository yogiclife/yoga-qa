**Original Question:**  
Clarification by acharya ji.

**Full Answer:**  
3. If Rama steals something from Shyama it is not that by so doing Rama causes an indentation in Shyama's mind and because of that has to experience an indentation of equal force in his own mind. "Indentation" in the mind is brought about only via one's own independent action. When the theft occurred, Shyama did not "do" anything; the action happened "to" him. Hence there is no indentation in Shyama's mind due to the theft. He experienced duhkha no doubt; but that duhkha is not an indentation in his mind. Only doers of action get an indentation in their mind as a result of the action they took.
