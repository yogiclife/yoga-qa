**Original Question:**  
Clarification by acharya ji.

**Full Answer:**  
2. There were several times during the session when I got the sense that what was being explained vis-a-vis punishment by Prakrti is that Prakrti punishes independent actions that go against the desire of PP, i.e. go against what PP wants. That is, bad actions. But it should be made clear here that good actions such as punya (paropakAra) are also punished by Prakrti. Both punya and papa are punished by Prakrti with samskara and ultimately phalabhoga. The only difference is that if one does punya then the reaction will be good and if one does papa then the reaction will be bad. But in both cases there will be reaction. Both are bandhana, both put one in chains. The chains of punya are chains of gold whereas the chains of papa are chains of iron; but both are chains and both cause rebirth. The only way not to get the punishment of Prakrti is to do action without being the doer; one feels that one is the tool or hand of PP. And that can only be the case when one is engaged in dharmic works, as only then can one's action be the desire of PP.
