**Original Question:**  
The question was whether the mind attains moksha (not mukti) or the unit consciousness. You mentioned that the mind ceases to exist and the unit consciousness then merges in the cosmic consciousness. Here is my understanding. Underlying the unit mind is purusha + some gunas and samskaras. On moksha, the samskaras get extinguished. Am I right that the gunas too leave the part of the purusha that was the mind and so that part of the purusha also merges in the cosmic consciousness? In mukti, samskaras get extinguished, but the gunas corresponding the cosmic mind remain, and so it just becomes a part of the cosmic mind.



**Full Answer:**  
What you say is true Mahodaya. The mind merges into Nirguna Purusa, and that is what moksa is. The jiivatma is already Nirguna Purusa, just in superficial bondage due to being linked with anumana.
