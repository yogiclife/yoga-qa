**Original Question:**  
We will learn dhyana at the shibiram. By practicing dhyana, we will get moksha (doing jagat hita in addition). Since we don’t require any additional knowledge for moksha after learning dhyana, I was wondering why there is a need for taraka brahma to give us guidance.

**Full Answer:**  
Taraka Brahma is the Entity who guides in dhyana. Without Him, no one can do dhyana.
