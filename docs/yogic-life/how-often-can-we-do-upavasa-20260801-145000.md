**Original Question:**  
Clarification by acharya ji.

**Full Answer:**  
Today is Amavasya in both India and USA. One can practice upavasa two times per month or four times per month. Two times is very good especially for starting with. For those who become more and more involved in the Yogic Life, four times a month is very good. All are welcome to join and do amavasya and purnima nirjalopasa as well if you would like. Just letting you know this is an option. Last week (Monday) I did upavasa, and today again six days later. Ekadashii and Amavasya/Purnima upavasa should be separated by a minimum of three days for taking usual food. So if one practices Ekadashii upavasa on Monday for example, the soonest one can do Amavasya/Purnima upavasa would be Friday. On the calendar, there are usually three or four days in between the two upavasa days.
