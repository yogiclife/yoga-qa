**Full Answer:**  
- One person asked, if one is already following sattvika ahara, then is this cleaning process after ekadashii upavasa really needed? What you replied was very good, that there are lots of twists and turns in the intestines and everything does not get cleared out during normal daily shaoca. That is true. But to make the point very, very clear it is good to add the following: So the day of ekadashii upavasa one has normal shaoca in the morning, and then one has nothing to eat or drink the entire day. Then the next day when one does the breaking process, so much comes out. Try it and see! By this it is very well illustrated and indeed proven, that even after daily shaoca a lot remains inside. Anyone who tries this breaking process after having had normal shaoca and then not eaten for 24 hours will be able to understand very clearly what it is that remains inside after daily shaoca. ‎<This message was edited>
