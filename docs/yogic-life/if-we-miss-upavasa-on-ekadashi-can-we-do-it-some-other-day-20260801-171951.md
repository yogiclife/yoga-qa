**Original Question:**  
One more question was, if someone is not available for the ekadashi upavasa, due to some other schedules, can they still do the upavasa and cleaning process on another day. I have answered, yes, we can do on the full moon / new moon day. Is it correct? 

**Full Answer:**  
Yes, if one cannot do nirjalopavasa on the three days of ekadashii, one may shift to purnima/amavasya. (This is mainly for new people. We just want them to get started so they can get established in twice-monthly upavasa. Really speaking, it is not the case that over the course of three days of ekadashii one cannot find even one day to do nirjalopavasa. Once one gets established in the practice one will be able to do it on one of the three days. Three days is plenty, one of the three can work for everyone.)
