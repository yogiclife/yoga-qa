**Original Question:**  
If those residing in the same house do not follow the same rules, is it important to keep separate utensils?

**Full Answer:**  
There are many households where different people continue for years to eat different food. So my reply about utensils is that absolutely, when people are eating meat/dairy/onion/garlic/mushrooms/oil, then utensils should be carefully kept separate. The point of sugar and salt is not as critical regarding utensils, as these two items do wash off completely, even with water. But the other items do not and so where there are different eating practices in the same household, separate utensils should be strictly maintained.
