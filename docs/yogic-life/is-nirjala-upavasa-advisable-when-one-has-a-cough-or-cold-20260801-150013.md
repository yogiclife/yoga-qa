**Original Question:**  
Is Nirjala upawasa advisable even if one has a cough and cold ?

**Full Answer:**  
In general, practicing upavasa is extremely good for health, and that includes for healing from illness, including mild illness such as cough and cold. It depends ultimately on how you feel, but getting better from a cold should be supported/expedited by practicing upavasa.
