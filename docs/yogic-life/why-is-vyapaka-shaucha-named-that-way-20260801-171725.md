**Original Question:**  
Namaskar Aacharya ji. In the last class, someone has raised this question, why the sanskrit term vyapak sauch is used for the cleaning process. 

**Full Answer:**  
- After doing vyapaka shaoca one feels a freshness in one's entire existence, entire body and mind, hence vyapaka shaoca.
