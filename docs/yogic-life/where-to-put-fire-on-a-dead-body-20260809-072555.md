**Original Question:**  
Namaskar acharya ji,



Why did you say that during cremation burning the fire in the mouth is disrespectful. It can be done on any part of the body so why does it matter from where it is done. So if we are saying mouth why it's disrespectful.



**Full Answer:**  
Yes, good question my brother. So a person's face is the most personal part of their body-- the face is where we express ourselves, how we know each other, the place from where we speak and see. It is the body part which gives us the closest identification and relation with the person whom we used to know. So when family and friends see a fire being placed directly into that person's mouth which is an integral part of their face, it is deeply painful and totally unnecessary.
