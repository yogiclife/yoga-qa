**Original Question:**  
Namaskar Mahodaya,

One of the student in my class is Type -1 Diabetic and this is what she has to say as she could try her upavasa. How do I suggest her to go about upavasa with her Diabetes... ?

**Full Answer:**  
Namaskar Bhagini,

So with the most common conditions-- hypertension (BP), cholesterol, type 2 diabetes -- people can just skip their medications the day of upavasa and they'll be fine. Type 1 diabetes is the most sensitive of the various chronic diseases. Although even here, most likely she can just not use any insulin that day and she will most probably do fine. In the absence of both food and insulin injections, her blood sugar will most likely be stable throughout the day. She should just check her blood sugar a few times to ensure it isn't going low. If it is a bit high that is fine-- 200-300 no worries. But if it is under 100, then she should be careful. If it drops under 90, then she should take some food. Low blood sugar is the main thing we would be concerned about-- if it were to go too low she would lose consciousness. So long as her blood sugar is above 100, then she can do nirjalopavasa and she will benefit a lot from it.
