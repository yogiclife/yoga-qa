**Original Question:**  
Clarification by acharya ji.

**Full Answer:**  
- Regarding breaking fast: 

- one liter of salt water is never enough. I never in 40 years saw anyone get full cleaning with one liter. One must continue taking salt water until full cleaning is achieved. "Some people may feel like having 2 liters or 3 liters" -- this is not the proper way to guide them. Rather, tell them that one is to continue until full cleaning is achieved. Minimum 2 liters salt water, usually 3 or 4 liters will be needed. I always use 4 liters, then a 5th of shuddha jal.

- For most people it will take minimum 45 minutes before cleaning starts. And total time for cleaning will be 2 hours minimum, first time will take 3-4 hours.

- Need to explain graphically about the stool so that they can understand-- first the stool will be solid, then liquid but with smell, then after multiple times it will become clear with no small or color. They need to understand that there is a whole process here. You told it so briefly and indirectly that many will not get the picture of what is involved. And when you had them repeat back to you then they also skipped over this whole prakriya. They need to understand that they will drink a liter of salt water, then go to the bathroom, then drink another liter, then go to the bathroom. In this way it will continue for several rounds. 

- Must explain proactively about what happens with too much or too little salt. Don't wait for someone to ask. In the first class you said at the end that you will explain about this next week. But it only takes 10 seconds to explain-- if too much salt you will get nauseated and maybe vomit, and if too little then the water will get absorbed and be evacuated via urine rather than stool. Everyone needs to understand this very clearly before trying to break for the first time.

- You have told the students 1 tsp of salt. But for most people 1.5 to 2 tsp salt will be needed in each liter. 

- Drink one liter of salt water every 15-30 minutes.
