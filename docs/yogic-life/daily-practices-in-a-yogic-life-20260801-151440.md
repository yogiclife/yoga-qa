**Original Question:**  
Clarification by acharya ji.

**Full Answer:**  
Namaskar to All,

Here is the list of daily practices we learned at the shivir which all need to incorporate into their daily lives. Now is the time!



1. Sadhana: 2x/day morning and evening, minimum 20 minutes, followed by guru puja, done on an empty stomach.

2. Asanas: 2x/day morning and evening, as practiced every day at the shivir: two yogic dances (minimum 10 min and 5 min), sarvangasana (2 min x 3), matsyamudra (1 min x 3), cakrasana/naokasana (30 sec x 4), utkatpashcimottanasana (8 sec, 8x), (sisters: diirghapranama (8x), yogamudra (8x), bhujangasana (8x)), matsyendrasana (30 sec x 4), gomukhasana (30 sec x 4), tuladandasana (30 sec x 4), mayurasana (30 sec x 4), body massage, shavasana. Using a timer is a must.

3. Vyapaka Shaoca: before sadhana, asanas, food, sleep. Also very good any time feeling sleepy during the day.

4. No daytime sleeping

5. Daily shaoca: minimum once on awakening, should be 3-5x/day if taking proper food. Clean using water; toilet paper prohibited.

6. Urination: To be done sitting down. Both males and females: use water to clean afterward.

7. Food: WFPB, No SOS

8. Nirjalopavasa: Twice per month on ekadashi, optionally four times/mo adding amavasya and purnima. Follow by full cleaning the next morning.

9. Daily run: all must exercise daily. If you choose to walk, it needs to be vigorous so that you are sweating when you finish. If you didn't sweat, you didn't exercise.

10. Inspire all those with whom you have contact in your life to follow these guidelines of the yogic life.
