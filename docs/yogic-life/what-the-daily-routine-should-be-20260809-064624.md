**Original Question:**  
 Is the timing of activities, siuch as when we wake up, when we eat different meals, when we exercise, and when we sleep, important? If so, can you suggest suitable times?

**Full Answer:**  
Your question regarding timing of activities, daily routine, is very good indeed, quite important. When you attend the upcoming shibiram you'll get a full and clear picture from direct experience as to how the daily routine is organized. You'll be learning sadhana as well as asanas, and we'll do all these as well as daily running, all together i.e. as a collective. So you'll get the full picture at that time. For now I'll say that early to bed and early to rise will give you an excellent start. For example, sleeping at 10:30 pm and awakening at 4:40 am.
