**Original Question:**  
Mahodaya, paragraph 17 of 'What is this world' mentions that "... every unit mind is a part of the cosmic mind". I assume that you don't mean that every unit mahattattva is a part of the cosmic mahattattva and every unit ahamtattva is a part of the cosmic ahamtattva, but rather that these are a part of the cosmic chitta. The reason is that the unit minds are a part of creation, and the entire creation is a part of the cosmic chitta. In fact, that unit  mind might have been a stone before, which is clearly in the cosmic chitta. Is this right?



**Full Answer:**  
I appreciate your question and the thought behind it. In reply I would say that the two scenarios you are describing are not separated one from the other in such a cut-and-dry way. On the one hand, yes it is the case that from 3:00 to 12:00 everything is occurring on the Cosmic Citta and as such, is the imagination of Cosmic Mind. The citta of Bhuma Man gets qualified by tamoguna and itself takes the forms of everything from akashatattva to ksititattva to one-celled organisms to human beings. So all that is certainly the case. And at the same time, Cosmic Citta is qualified Cosmic Ahamtattva which is qualified Cosmic Mahattattva which is qualified Nirguna Purusa. As we read back in "What is Dharma", each of these are functional forms of the same "I", the same single Paramatma. And so Cosmic Citta contains Cosmic Ahamtattva and Cosmic Mahattattva and Nirguna Purusa within it in potential form. Remember that "mind" is created only from 12:00 to 3:00 and nowhere else in srsticakra. When a single-celled organism emerges from ksititattva, only its citta is active but it has ahamtattva and mahattattva as well. And those ahamtattva and mahattattva are the very mahattattva and ahamtattva which were created at 2:00 and 1:00 respectively. And the mind of that very one-celled organism will gradually develop and become the mind of a human. And that human's mind contains citta, ahamtattva, and mahattattva. And those three have only been created from 1:00 to 3:00 in srsticakra, nowhere else. So in conclusion, to really understand the reply to your question it requires a melding of the two scenarios you painted-- for the reason which I have described here..
