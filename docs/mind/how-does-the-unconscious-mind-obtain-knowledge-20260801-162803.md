**Full Answer:**  
Namaskar X Mahodaya,

Listened to your session, all excellent. Y ji asked several good questions, and your answers were detailed, deep, and right on the mark. 

- Near the beginning where you were explaining about conscious and unconscious mind and how it comes about that people get answers to their inner questions, just one point I wanted to make sure is clear: when someone is sitting in front of a murti or a picture which they consider to be "God", and their mind becomes very concentrated due to severe anxiety about something happening in their life, or due to some point which they have a deep desire to know about, then due to the resultant concentration of mind they may actually get the answer for their anxiety or for the point they wanted to know. And they attribute it to the murti in front of them, thinking it to be God. What I understood you to explain in correction of this was that the person did not get the answer from God, but rather from having come in connection with the unconscious mind within themselves which is sarvajina as it is itself linked with the universal consciousness. So all that is true-- just want to clarify that it is not that they did not get the answer from God-- they did indeed get the answer from God. The point, as I think you do already know, is that the murti or the picture sitting before them is not but just some mundane object like any other stone or any other picture, and as such, being jada it can neither give any blessing nor provide any answer or any solution. But the way they got the answer was indeed via God (Brahma, Parama Purusa) who is within them (rather than in front of them) and with whom they came in contact via their own unconscious mind. The unconscious mind is sarvajina only because it is linked with Parama Purusa, or the universal consciousness. The main point here is that the unconscious mind cannot know anything independently of Parama Purusa. And again, this is not a state of high realization, it is merely that due to their anxiety the mind become temporarily concentrated and so they got access to their unconscious mind which is linked with the Infinite Source of all knowledge. But this is not an elevated state.
