**Original Question:**  
How many vrittis are there?

**Full Answer:**  
The 1000 vrttis are not derived from 50 vrttis by 10 indriyas, but by 50 vrttis going in 10 directions (N,S,E,W,NW,SW,NE,SE,Up,Down), and then by two-- introversial (done in the mind) and extroversial (done in the world). The sister who presented after you recited it back in the same way,  so this needs to be corrected. The number 1000 vrttis has nothing to do with there being 10 indriyas.
