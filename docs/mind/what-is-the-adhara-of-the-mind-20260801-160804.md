**Original Question:**  
Mahodaya, paragraph 26 of the handout 'What is my relation with the universe and the cosmic entity?' mentions that 'The reaction should have made the mind regains its normal form, but due to death it remains incomplete and takes the shelter of unit consciousness at the time of death as a potential force or energy (samskara)'. Is this saying that the samskara [reaction in potential form] takes the shelter of unit consciousness? I assume this means that the the jeevaatmaa is the aadhaara for samskaara. Isn't the mind the aadhaara for samskaara?



[Follow up] Mahodaya, can you clarify the above question that I had asked a while back? A student asked me a question on this today.

**Full Answer:**  
What you told X ji in reply was correct. Everything in this universe needs an adhara. The only entity which is niradhara is  Brahma. The mind takes the body as its adhara during life; after death the mind no longer has the body has its adhara and so it needs a new adhara. Its adhara becomes jiivatma which, itself being shuddha purusa, is niradhara. And, although the mind does indeed have an adhara even after death, that adhara does not permit of the facility of activity of mind. Mind is linked with jiivatma but as such becomes inactive until such a time when it again gets the body as an adhara at which time it will again become active.
