**Original Question:**  
Paragraph 25 mentions that 'In order to take the form of a crude object chitta has to change from subtle to crude.' Does the chitta actually become crude or have the appearance of being crude? The reason for my question is that I thought the mind was subtle and would not have tanmatras. While the real book generates tanmatras and is perceived by the indriyas, I thought the form of a book taken by the chitta does not generate tanmatras and is perceived by the ahamtattva without needing tanmatras. Since indriyas don't grasp the form of the book taken by the chitta, I thought that form is not crude, but only has the appearance of being crude.



**Full Answer:**  
Good question Mahodaya. In reply I'll mention three things:

1) We know that actually nothing in the universe is crude as everything is made of purusa. The objects of the world appear to be crude but are not actually so. Everything is occurring in the mind of Saguna Brahma.

2) It is nonetheless true that the objects of the world which are external to us i.e. outside of our mind, are indriya-grahya and we accept them in our everyday life as crude -- whereas the objects imagined in our mind are not indriya-grahya and are not crude in the way that the objects outside of us are. That having been said, there is meaning in calling the imagined book in our mind as crude; if we go on thinking about (i.e. seeing) the book in our mind for our whole life, our mind will become crude and we will be born in our next life as a book. So the image of a book in our mind has the vibration of something crude, due to which our mind becomes crude due to thinking about it.

3) As with some other subjects discussed and explained in earlier sections of the karapatrams, this is a model, an analogy to help us understand. We can easily conceive of the example of Rama sitting in Prayag and imagining Chandni Chowk, and that indeed Rama is thinking of something crude and by doing so he creates something which has reality value inside his mind-- on the basis of which we can then understand how SB can imagine something on Its citta on the basis of which this imagined thing takes on reality value within the mind of SB -- which is for us the objective world. And so the model works well in this capacity, as we move at each step from the known to the unknown.
