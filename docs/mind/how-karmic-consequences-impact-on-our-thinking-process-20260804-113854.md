**Original Question:**  
How karmic consequences impact on our thinking process, how yad bhaavam tad bhavati will work there?

**Full Answer:**  
t karmic consequences impact on our thinking process, how yad bhaavam tad bhavati will work there. What we think and do affects what we become. As one thinks, so one becomes. The mind has no shape of its own and takes the shape of that which it is engaged in. So if the mind is engaged in crude things, it becomes those very things. And by that, the mind gets profoundly degenerated. This is the fundamental principal behind the yogic life. By engaging in high-minded things -- sadhana and seva -- the mind gets elevated and ultimately becomes one with Parama Purusa. And on the other side, by engaging in crude activities, the mind degenerates and as a result one will get reborn as a stone, or a mosquito, or as a snail or other undeveloped creature.
