**Original Question:**  
Clarification by acharya ji.

**Full Answer:**  
Namaskar X ji,

Yah bat jo maine yahan niche likha hai, vah ek samanya nirdesh hai-- 

- Sattvaguna se manasik shanti

- Rajoguna se manasik caincalata

- Tamoguna se manasik alasya



Kintu yah bhi samjho ki ye jo niche diye gaye che dosh hain, ye sab tamoguna ke antargat hain--



षड्दोषाः पुरुषेणेह हातव्या भूतिमिच्छता ।

निद्रा तन्द्रा भयं क्रोध आलस्यं दीर्घसूत्रता ॥



Ye sare tamoguna ke antargat hain: nidra, tandra, bhay, krodh, diirghasutrata (procrastination/delay psychology)



To krodh jo hai vah rajogunii chiiz nahiin hai; tamogunii hi hai.
