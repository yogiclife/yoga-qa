**Original Question:**  
NamaskaraH, mahodaya. A student asked me about whether near death experiences were real. I confirmed that promised to explain more about it next time. In response to my question on how those experiencing NDE are able to see their body without indriyas, you had mentioned that "The mind can also be divided into conscious, subconscious, and unconscious aspects or parts. Although the conscious mind has direct access only to information it gains via the indriyas, the unconscious mind is linked with the vast consciousness and sadhakas may gain access to whole areas of knowledge (past lives, intuitionally gained knowledge etc). Young children (under 5) may also have access to some parts of their mind which they lose access to later on, and those having near-death experiences may also similarly get temporary access to parts of their mind which they did not have access to in their daily lives." From what I understand, when we are awake, our conscious mind is active. When we are dreaming, our subconscious mind is active. In high spiritual states or sadhakas or NDE, the unconscious mind is active. Is this understanding correct? Is the unconscious mind connected with the cosmic mind, which enables it to access all knowledge in the cosmic mind? In deep sleep, are we still at the level of subconscious mind or are we at the level of unconscious mind? When people with NDE come back to the conscious state, are they able to remember things because the knowledge from the unconscious mind seeped back into the conscious mind? So, have their conscious chittas taken shapes that were originally accessible only to the unconscious mind?

**Full Answer:**  
Namaskar Mahodaya,



Listened to both your sessions-- the one from last week as well as the one from this week. Everything you explained to them was good. 

- With regard to NDE, what you said was correct. And your understanding of conscious, subconscious, and unconscious mind is proper.

- In response to your questions above: unconscious mind is connected with Cosmic Mind, yes. And it is always active; it is not that it is only active at certain times such as deep sleep or NDE. It is just that, due to the dominance of conscious mind, one doesn't normally have access to the unconscious mind.

- One gets access to the unconscious mind during deep sleep as the other two parts of mind are not active. But usually upon awakening, one does not remember what one had access to. On rare occasion one does.

- In elevated states of sadhana one gets access to the unconscious mind, and there one will remember clearly after the sadhana session is complete.

- In other situations, the mind may become one-pointed and focused due to the pressure of the circumstance, and as a result may get access to one's unconscious mind. These are not situations in which the mind is elevated, but just it gets concentrated due to the situation. The NDE is one example. Another example is where a person is extremely worried about something, and may be sitting, praying in front of a murti. Due to extreme worry about something in one's life the mind gets concentrated and apexed and one gets access to the unconscious mind as a result, and gets some solution to one's problem -- and mistakenly attributes the solution to the blessing of the stone murti. Whereas in reality it had nothing at all to do with the murti, but was rather a temporary access to one's own unconscious mind due to one's extreme anxiety. This is not at all an elevated state.

- "In deep sleep, are we still at the level of subconscious mind or are we at the level of unconscious mind?" Unconscious mind. "When people with NDE come back to the conscious state, are they able to remember things because the knowledge from the unconscious mind seeped back into the conscious mind?" Yes. "So, have their conscious chittas taken shapes that were originally accessible only to the unconscious mind?" Yes.
