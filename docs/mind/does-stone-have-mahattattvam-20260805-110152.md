**Original Question:**  
Does stone have mahattattvam?

**Full Answer:**  
 It is not true that stone has mahattattva. Stone does not have its own mind, so there is no question of its having mahattattva. It does not have mahattattva, ahamtattva, or citta. It is made of Cosmic Mind yes, but that is a very different thing; it does not have mind i.e. does not have anumana. There is a huge difference between being made of mind, and having mind. Under conducive conditions, jiiva emerges from stone-- and therein we get the emergence of anumana. Once anumana emerges with the one-celled organism, then only do we get mind and yes it has all three parts of mind within it.
