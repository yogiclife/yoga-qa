**Original Question:**  
Whether the mirror analogy indicates that both the reflected jiivatma and also therefore the mind derived from it are not real, just as the image in a mirror is not real.



**Full Answer:**  
The purpose of the mirror analogy is to explain how it is that the mahattattva of the jiiva has a reflection of Paramatma in it, the reflection being jiivatma. It is analogous to having one hundred mirrors on the periphery of a circle, each reflecting a flower located at the center of the circle. That is the purpose of the analogy; the purpose ends there. The fact that the reflection in a mirror is only a virtual existence is not used in the analogy. Every analogy has its purpose and its limitation. The limitation of the analogy lies in the fact that the reflected image of the flower is only a virtual image, whereas jiivatma is not a virtual image; it truly exists. So Sanjiv Mahoday needs to understand this, and then his confusion about what is real and what is not real will be finished. As, that consideration is not at all part of the mirror analogy, whose purpose is exclusively for showing what is meant by the reflection of Paramatma in the mahattattva.
