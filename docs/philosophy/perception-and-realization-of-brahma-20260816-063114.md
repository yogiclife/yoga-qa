**Original Question:**  
How to perceive and realize Brahma?



In light of this infinite nature of brahman, can you clarify if my following understanding of Shiva's and Krishna's appearance in the world is correct? Taraka brahma is also brahma, and so they were/are both infinite. When they appeared in the world too, they were infinite but manifested in the visible shape of a human, like an LCD screen may display some small figures on the screen, but the liquid crystal is there throughout the screen. It is just being make visible in some parts of the screen temporarily. But it was there in the whole screen before the appearance of the figures and will remain on the screen after the disappearance too.



**Full Answer:**  
This is addressing the difference between intellectual conceptualization of something vs actual realization of something. It is true that 'Mind can never think, feel or grasp any idea of the size of Brahma.' It is also true that through a process of rational thought, the mind can reach the conclusion that Brahma is infinite, even though the mind cannot truly grasp the idea of what it means for something to be infinite. That only happens through spiritual realization. The statement in paragraph 9 related not merely to direct perception but to realization. Whereas the anumana in paragraph 10 uses logic. We can logically deduce something without have direct realization of it.



The sense here is that the biggest that something can be without being infinite or virat, is vishala. There is nothing which is not infinite and yet bigger than the srsti. Hence the srsti is vishala. And there is only one thing which is bigger than vishala, and that is virat, or infinite. As the srsti is within Brahma, Brahma must be virat, infinite.



(We know that the srsti is not infinite as it is created, and yet we are unable to measure its size and hence it is vishala.)



Ultimately the infinite nature of Brahma must be realized through sadhana. And yet taking a rational approach to its assessment in the plane of the mind is extremely valuable for helping people come to understand what is the proper path.



The main thing for people to understand is that Shiva and Krsna are not that physical body but the mind inhabiting that body. That mind is all-pervading both during the time It is present in that physical body and during the time it is not present in that body.
