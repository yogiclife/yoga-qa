**Original Question:**  
"How does crude matter arise out of subtle mind". 

**Full Answer:**  
"How does crude matter arise out of subtle mind". We discussed the need to use the analogy of butter in milk to bring out the important nuance involved in the above question. In order to produce butter from milk, butter needs to be present in milk. 



You used this butter analogy to say that just as the seed of butter is present in milk and therefore butter can be made from milk, in the same way the seed of the crude creation is present in Cosmic Mind and hence the crude paincabhuta are able to be made from it. Whereas in reality it is the opposite: In contrast to the case of butter in which its seed is present in milk, the seed of the crude creation is not at all present in Cosmic Mind as mind is subtle, and hence how is it possible that the crude universe could be created from it? This is the dilemma that is presented.



In the karapatram it says, "In the same way we get butter from milk only because it existed in milk in another form. If the seed of the crude universe existed in Him, Puruśa could not be called subtle or understandable only as an idea."



Here in the karaptram butter made from milk is used to expose an apparent contradiction: If purusa is really and truly subtle, then it cannot contain the seed of the crude universe within it. And yet if the seed did not exist in purusa, how could the universe have been created?



Ultimately we come to the conclusion that as purusa and Cosmic Mind are subtle, the seed of the universe could not be present within them. Yet it is a fact that the crude universe has been created. And so the only way it could have come into existence, given that its seed form is not present in them, is that the universe is created as an imagination. 



From the karapatram:



"Here again a contradictory situation arises. It was said earlier that the universe has been created out of Puruśa, but if the seed did not exist in Puruśa, how could the universe have been created? This sounds illogical and unreasonable, and the only logical thing to say is that the universe was never created, as Puruśa is subtle by nature and the crude universe could not have been created out of Him. It was however said earlier that the universe was created out of Him, and as it has been logically proved to be true, the only other rational statement would be to say that the crude universe was never a created reality. Yet the existence of this visible universe cannot be ignored. In fact, this crude universe is created only as a thought-projection of Puruśa."



The crude universe is not present in seed form in Purusa or Cosmic mind, as they are subtle, whereas the universe is crude. Hence the butter & milk analogy does not work here, and the only solution is that the universe is created as a thought-projectin only. That is why it is not sufficient to simply explain how the crude universe arises from Cosmic Mind by going through the stepwise creation of paincabhuta with the increase of tanmatras at each step, etc. To only explain that is to miss the main point that the paincabhuta were not created as a reality, as to have done so would have required that the seed of the crude universe be present in Cosmic Mind which it is not-- and hence the only way the crude paincabhuta could be created from the Cosmic Mind, itself being subtle, is rather only as an imagination.
