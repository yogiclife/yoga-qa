**Original Question:**  
am I right in understanding that Brahma is simultaneously in a saguna and nirguna state? That is the only way I am able to reconcile both states of brahma being anadi. This looks contradictory, but I guess it is beyond karya-karana bhava, where contradictions don’t arise. 



In my notes, I have written that these are like two roles of the same brahma (since there is only one brahma). However, mutually exclusive roles cannot occur simultaneously. So, the only possibility is that these roles are not mutually exclusive.



**Full Answer:**  
At any given time, some parts of Brahma are in the nirguna state and some parts are in the saguna state. It is like the ocean being nirguna purusa with some icebergs interspersed throughout the ocean. The icebergs are saguna.

[5/8/25, 9:38:05 PM] Swarup Mohdaya - Yoga: Srsti has always been going on and will never end, but everything within it has a discrete beginning and end-- and for that reason is neither considere as anadi nor as ananta.
