**Original Question:**  
Paragraph 26 mentions 'Chitta creates the result of the action of aham tattva and thereby obtains objectivity, either subtle or crude'. Am I right in understanding that the objectivity here refers to srishti, subtle to the unit mind, and crude to the all the crude substances in the world? Thanks.



**Full Answer:**  
Yes Mahodaya, your understanding is correct. I would just add that as we know, there is a whole range of degrees of crudity in which, in a relative sense, the term subtle can also be used. So at one extreme there is the unit mind which is entirely subtle, and then there is the range extending from akashatattva to ksititattva each containing relative crudity and relative subtlety. But yes, in summary what you have stated is correct.
