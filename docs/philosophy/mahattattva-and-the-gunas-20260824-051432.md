**Original Question:**  
Mahodaya, your explanation of paragraph 26 mentioned 'In every phenomenon, all three gunas are present, but one always dominates. Here, sattva initiates the creation of cosmic mind by forming the mahat tattva, or the intellect.' Does this mean that mahattattva actually has some rajas and tamas, albeit in small quantities?



**Full Answer:**  
What I wrote regarding all three gunas being present with one dominating-- this applies to the created world from 3:00 to 12:00. The creation of mind does not function in that way; mahattattva is created purely by the effect of sattvaguna on shuddha purusa and as rajas and tamas are not yet active there, mahattattva is purely the result of sattvaguna.
