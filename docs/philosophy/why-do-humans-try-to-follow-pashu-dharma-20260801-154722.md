**Full Answer:**  
And with regard to the follow-up point which Sabya Mahodaya and I had discussed by phone after his last class: At the beginning of his last class, Mahodaya nicely explained about nimittamatra. There was just one point which needed further explanation, and that was that he mentioned that humans have the option to follow manava dharma, or, due to the fact that they have free will, they can follow and abuse pashu dharma. The way Mahodaya explained it, it sounded like human beings' inability to follow pashu dharma and, for all those humans who default to pashu dharma, their inevitable abuse of pashu dharma, is a direct result of humans' faculty of free will. But the faculty of free will is not the cause of humans' abuse of pashu dharma. It is very important to to understand and to explain clearly to students that:

- The great majority of humans default to the path of following pashu dharma as, due to their own lengthy history of living in animal form, it is the known path, the familiar path.

- However, as we know, the unique characteristic of human beings is that they all have within them an infinite desire. Some are aware of the fact; most have no idea about this. They do not know that they have an infinite desire, and so when they default to the path of animal dharma, they do so trying to fulfill an infinite desire which they do not understand that they have. 

- They are trying to fulfill their infinite desire through finite means, and this leads to inevitable abuse of all four elements of pashu dharma, plus all the ancillary finite things of this world. This all comes within pashu dharma, and it is inevitably abused. The reason for the abuse is the mismatch between the infinite desire within them, and the finite nature of the things they are trying to fulfill it with.

- Hence the main conclusion: The cause of humans' abuse of pashu dharma is their unawareness of and inability to understand and address the infinite desire within them. The cause is this alone, and not their faculty of free will. Free will is what allows them to opt for the path of animal life, but lack of understanding of the infinite desire within them and their inability to address it is what leads to their inevitable abuse of pashu dharma.
