**Original Question:**  
Manava Dharma

**Full Answer:**  
The dharma of humans is a life dedicated to fulfillment of one's infinite desire by all-round effort to merge with the Infinite Entity.  I just want to emphasize that this exclusively is manava dharma; there are not any other gunas included.  Knowledge and creativity are not part of the qualitative difference between humans and animals. Not all humans have much knowledge, and all animals have some knowledge-- so knowledge is not a qualitative difference between humans and animals. And many humans are not at all creative. So creativity is not part of manava dharma or the qualitative difference between humans and animals. Manava dharma is related exclusively with the pursuit of the Infinite Entity. Nothing else.
