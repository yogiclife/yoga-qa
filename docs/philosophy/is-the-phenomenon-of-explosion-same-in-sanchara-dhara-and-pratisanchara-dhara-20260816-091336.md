**Original Question:**  
Is the phenomenon of explosion same in Sanchara dhara and Pratisanchara dhara ?

**Full Answer:**  
- A couple of times during the session today you described the phenomenon of "explosion" as being common to pratisaincara and negative saincara alike, but that is not the case. 



It is not that the same explosion of ksititattva results either in (1) life emerging from stone or in (2) backward movement dispersing into the 5 fundamental factors (paincabhuta) depending upon whether there are conducive conditions present for life. 

What you have described with the initial increasing pressure of tamoguna on ksititattva is indeed true for both pathways. 

But the end result in the case of life is a subtle emergence, not an explosion. Whereas in the case of negative pratisaincara there is an actual explosion, known as visphota.



The reason for the difference is that the step immediately preceding the ultimate result is, in the case of negative saincara, excessive pressure of tamoguna, whereas in the case of prati-saincara, it is the change in dominance from tamoguna to sattvaguna. Excessive pressure of tamoguna results in an explosion, whereas change to dominance of sattvaguna results in the subtle process of the emergence of living beings.



- In response to the student's question, there is indeed more to be said about how life emerges. One needs to explain about केन्द्रातीगा शक्ति, केन्द्रानुगा शक्ति, and प्राणाः, which is not an introductory subject but can certainly be explained in detail should the need arise.
