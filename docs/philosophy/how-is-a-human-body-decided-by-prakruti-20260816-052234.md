**Full Answer:**  
If someone has been born in a human body then they have a human mind. And the definition of a human mind is a mind having infinite desire. 

If a particular jiiva is not yet ready to have free will and an infinite desire, then Prakruti will not give them a human body. And if there ever were such a human i.e. who had only finite desire and as such did not abuse the things of this world as, like animals, the person was satisfied with finite amounts of food, money etc, then they would be categorized rather as an animal. 

And the person having an infinite desire but trying to satisfy it with finite things would be worse than them and not better, because such a person is not merely an animal in human form but is worse than an animal as they would be degenerating themselves on a daily basis with their crude activities such that at the time of death they would no longer qualify for even an animal birth and would rather be ready only for the life of an insect or a stone. Such persons are पशोः अधमः । Rather they are अधमाधमः ।
