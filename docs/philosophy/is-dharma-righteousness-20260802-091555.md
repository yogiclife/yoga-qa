**Original Question:**  
 Does dharma mean righteousness?

**Full Answer:**  
On the point of the one brother's question regarding dharma meaning "righteousness". You were correct that this definition applies to humans only and is therefore not a universal definition. Furthermore, it is actually simply a natural outcome (one of many) of following the true manava dharma. When one leads a life dedicated to realization of the Infinite, a natural outcome of this life is that it is a moral life. And indeed in yoga, as you know we dive deep into the subject of morality through yama and niyama. So we are very, very clear and specific about what a moral life constitutes. Whereas this term "righteousness" is something which (a) is not clear as a term, and also (b) even if we accept the term, it has no legs to stand on in the absence of a life which is dedicated to pleasing Parama Purusa with one's every action. This is the difference between simple morality and spiritual morality. Simple morality has no foundation to stand on, whereas spiritual morality has a firm base-- that of Parama Purusa.
