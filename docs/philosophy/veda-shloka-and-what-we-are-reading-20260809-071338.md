**Original Question:**  
Mahodaya, we have been studying some verses from the Vedas. What is yoga’s position on the Vedas?

**Full Answer:**  
Namaskar Mahodaya,

Each shloka in the Vedas is taken on its own merits. In the Jinana Kanda there are many shlokas which are very  good and provide guiding light in spiritual life and the path of manava dharma.
