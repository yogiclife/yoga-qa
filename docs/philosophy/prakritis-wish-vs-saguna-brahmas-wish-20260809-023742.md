**Original Question:**  
Paragraph 7 of 'What is this world?' mentions that 'Similarly, Prakriti gives all these shapes to Purusha according to Her wish to create this universe'. I thought that we discussed earlier that prakriti does not have an independent wish, and just does what brahma wants her to do. In the sentence here, do we mean that prakriti's wish is just to carry out saguna brahma's wish to create this universe. So, it is not an independent desire on the part of prakriti?



**Full Answer:**  
Prakrti (Shakti) is a force who acts as she has been instructed to act by Purusa (Shiva). Here "according to Her wish" means in accordance with how She has been "designed" to act. So yes, the way you have worded it is correct.



Yes, this is a very nice subject. So the sperm and the egg are both alive and as such, they each have their own mind and jiivatma. When the sperm joins with the egg a new jiiva is born. This is called conception. With the joining of the sperm and the egg, the genetic material of both are combined together. This joining of the sperm and the egg results in a single cell, called a zygote, which contains the complete genetic information for the new individual. At the moment when the sperm and egg join, the life of each (i.e. the sperm and egg)  ends and the mind and jiivatma of both the sperm and egg leave and become bodiless minds, as when any living being dies. (At the time of any jiiva's death the unit mind and jiivatma leave the body and remain together as a unit, waiting to be assigned by Praktrti to a new body.) And at the same time as the life ends of the sperm and egg, the newly conceived zygote is the new single-celled life which will divide and multiply to become an embryo and ultimately a fetus. At the time of conception, with the formation of the zygote, Prakrti assigns a  bodiless unit mind with its jiivatma to that zygote. This assignment is done in such a way that the samskaras of that bodiless mind will have opportunity to be expressed in the environment into which the baby-to-be will be born. And so here is the answer to your student's question:  a unit consciousnesses and mind becomes linked to a baby’s body-to-be at the time of conception, at the moment of joining of the sperm and the egg. And really, to keep it simple this last sentence is all you need to tell the student unless you particularly wish to explain more.
