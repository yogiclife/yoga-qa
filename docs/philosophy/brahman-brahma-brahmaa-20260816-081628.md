**Original Question:**  
Brahman, Brahma, Brahmaa

**Full Answer:**  
And you explained the point nicely about ब्रह्मन्‌, ब्रह्म, ब्रह्मा ।  That is, the concept that one should refer to the Cosmic Entity as ब्रह्म rather than as ब्रह्मन्‌ or ब्रह्मा. I would just say that it will be good to emphasize more the reason why the pratipadikam "Brahman" cannot be used. And as you know, the reason is that it is the same pratipadikam for both brahma and brahmA. And thus, by saying "Brahman" it can indicate both brahma and brahmA and one cannot differentiate between them. You did mention this very briefly, but I do not think the students would have got it. Remember: they have years and years of galat abhyasa using the word "Brahman". Habits are not easily broken. They need to understand clearly why this word cannot be used, why it does not make any sense to use it. You can explain this point again, and have them repeat it. The brother Purusottama, when asked in session #16 to explain what he understood regarding this word, he did not include this point in his explanation. And that was the most important point. If one says "Brahman", one cannot have any idea whether they are referring to brahma or brahmA as it is the pratipadikam of both words.
