**Original Question:**  
Paragraph 4 mentions the unit consciousness as the knowing entity. I thought it was just the witness, but would not have knowledge. The aham tattva would be the entity that has knowledge. Can you clarify why the unit consciousness is called the knowing entity? 

**Full Answer:**  
The जीवात्मा is the ज्ञातृसत्ता. It is seeing everything and as such it is the "knowing entity". Just as Nirguna Brahma is beyond mind and yet is सर्वज्ञ.
