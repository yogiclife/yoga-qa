**Original Question:**  
Pashu Dharma is one comprising of four qualities

**Full Answer:**  
 Pashu-dharma is only one. It comprises of four qualities, but they are not each a separate dharma. The term "dharma" refers to that which makes a thing what it is, and without which that thing would not remain what it is. For animals, their dharma is bhaya, nidra, ahara, and maetuna. These four together constitute pashu-dharma. As such, pashu-dharma is only one, not four, and will never be used in the plural as "pashu dharmas".
