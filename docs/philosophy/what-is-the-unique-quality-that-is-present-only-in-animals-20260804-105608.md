**Original Question:**  
What is the unique quality that is present only in animals?

**Full Answer:**  
What is the unique quality that is present only in animals? If we speak about only food, sleep and fear and procreation. This looks like a common quality between human beings and animals. Excellent question. These four qualities are the gunas unique to animals.

So why are they present in humans? Because humans have come from animals, humans have evolved from animals, and as such these qualities remain in humans. And not only do they remain, but as you know there is a constant duel (fight) between the animal qualities and the human quality in each human being.



And I understand that the matter of humans having "come from" or "evolved from" animals is a sensitive matter for those who adhere to tenants of religion as religions teach that "God created the world" as it were, in a flash, and humans were there from the beginning. So you needn't emphasize this particular point of evolution, they will learn it slowly. But if you ever need to help them understand about this, you can ask them whether they accept that there were beings called "dinosaurs" on the planet earlier? And so many jiivas were there who are no longer here now, right? So not all jiivas are on the planet at the same time-- indeed there is an evolution of life forms. And this does not at all go against the concept that PP has create the universe, as indeed He has. This kramagata vikasha is the way He has done it.
