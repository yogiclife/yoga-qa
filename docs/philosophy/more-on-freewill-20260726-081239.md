**Original Question:**  
is my following definition of free will correct? Free will is when the mind has choices and the ability to select amongst the choices. In animals, the brain does the selection, and so prakriti is responsible. The mind did not make the choice, and so there is no free will. In humans, the mind (aham tattva in particular) makes the choice, and so the mind is responsible.



**Full Answer:**  
This is good. I would just add that "In animals, the brain does the selection *in accordance with its inborn instincts, i.e. vrttis which since the animal's birth are the basis of the brain's design and decision-making algorithm. These inborn instincts are instilled in the animal by prakrti,* and so prakrti is responsible."

Parama Purusa knows what is going to happen in the future. That does not mean that from PP's perspective human beings do not have free will. He has designed the human being to consider all the options he has before him at any moment in time and to then decide what he thinks is best. That is free will. From both the perspective of the human being and the perspective of Parama Purusa, from both perspectives it is free will. PP knows what the human being will choose, but that does not mean it is not free will. It is most definitely free will. PP has designed the plants and animals and created them in such a way that they do not have free will, they function on the basis of their inborn instincts. And in contrast, He knows that the hb's he has created have free will, indeed it is His design. So it is not that from PP's perspective human beings do not have free will; they most certainly do.



I would say here that there isn't value really in comparing this to the scientific viewpoint about predictability as science has no idea about mind or PP. What I expressed above is beyond the scope of science as science deals only with the indriya-grahya vishva. 



If we were to say that PP is beyond time and therefore His understanding does not relate with time, this would I think also not be accurate as He has created the world and is intimately familiar with the phenomenon of relativity, time-place-person, past-present-future. He created all of this, and certainly knows it and relates intimately with it. This entire srsti is in His mind, and so the concepts of past-present-future are also His own. PP knows everything that is going to happen, and also for PP there is free will for each and every one of His human beings. He does not tell hbs what to do; He lets each and every hb decide what they would like to do in each situation.
