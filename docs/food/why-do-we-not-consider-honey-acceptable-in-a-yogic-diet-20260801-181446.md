**Full Answer:**  
More regarding honey:

- Ethically, it is an animal product and thus involves exploitation. It is made by bees for their own consumption and use within the hive.

- Nutritionally, it is a sugar and so comes in the category of SOS. While it does contain tiny amounts of antioxidants, vitamins, and beneficial enzymes, these are present in small quantities, and honey is fundamentally a form of sugar.
