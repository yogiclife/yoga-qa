Namaskar X ji,

It is very good to see that you are actively reading and learning-- that is very good. And I can tell by your presentations that you have done a lot of reading and preparation. 



With regard to the concept of animal protein intake resulting in a heavy acid load for the kidneys that gets buffered by leaching calcium from the bones which results in osteoporosis-- this is a something which was researched and discussed a lot from 1980 to 2000, and I used to include it in my lectures in those days. The article you reference is from 1998. And indeed, it makes sense to me. Yet, I am not sure why but it hasn't been getting discussed or written about for the last twenty years or so. I will contact my colleagues at PCRM and see what they have to say about the status of research on this idea. If I find out anything more, will let you know.
