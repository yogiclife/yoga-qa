**Full Answer:**  
This is also true Mahodaya, yes-- it is an important part of the overall picture. Fresh whole fruits are a perfectly safe and healthy part of the WFPB diet for people with diabetes. No fruit juices, only the whole fruit. And we need to remember that their insulin sensitivity and over health condition will be improving every day. So in this setting especially, including whole fresh fruits is fine and indeed very good.
