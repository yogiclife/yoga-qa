**Full Answer:**  
- At the very end you said something about "lubrication", how the body needs "lubrication" and it gets that through the fat in avocados. Bhagini, this is all dogma. There is no such thing as lubrication. Engines made of steel need lubrication with oil. Seeing this, some got the idea that human beings also need "lubrication with oil". It is all fake, false, just dogma. There is no such thing as lubrication for the human body. What needs lubrication? The joints? Nothing of the sort. No one needs to eat avocado, no one needs to eat nuts. People can eat these items in limited amounts, but they should understand that they do not need to.
