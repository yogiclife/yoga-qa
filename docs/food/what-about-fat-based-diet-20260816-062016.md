**Original Question:**  
Is fat harmful for the body?

**Full Answer:**  
It is important to understand that this is not merely a casual preference. Fat is not intended to be the fuel for the body; it is intended as a tool for storing excess calories. Carbohydrate is what is intended as the fuel for the body; it is the intended input for the Krebs Cycle for energy production.  If fat is primarily used in the Krebs cycle instead of glucose, it can create a state of acidosis due to the production of ketone bodies as a byproduct of excessive fatty acid breakdown, which are acidic in nature. It is frankly dangerous, and we often have to admit people with diabetes to the hospital for this very reason-- often even to the ICU. The reason is that although they are eating carbohydrates, but due to a lack of insulin the glucose accumulates in the blood and is unable to be delivered to the organs of the body where it can be used. As a result, the cells throughout the body are starving for glucose and are forced to produce energy using fat, which creates a dangerous acidic state that people can even die from.
