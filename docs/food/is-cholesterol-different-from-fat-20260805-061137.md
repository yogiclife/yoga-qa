**Original Question:**  
Is cholesterol different from fat?

**Full Answer:**  
 Cholesterol is different from fat; it is not itself a fat but a separate compound. There is no nutritional need for cholesterol as the human body produces all the cholesterol it needs. Hence any cholesterol on eats is above and beyond what one needs and should be avoided. There is no cholesterol in any food that comes from a plant; it is only contained in animal products. If one gets a blood test done (fasting lipid panel), in the test result one will see separate listings for the blood levels of Total Cholesterol, HDL cholesterol, LDL cholesterol, and VLDL cholesterol. The HDL cholesterol is the "good cholesterol" as higher levels are good, whereas the LDL cholesterol is the "bad cholesterol" as higher levels are not good. Those eating a WFPD Diet with no SOS will naturally have very good levels of all these types of cholesterol, as they are allowing their body to manage its production which is how it is supposed to be.
