**Full Answer:**  
There are also some very trusted websites where you can get good information:

1. https://nutritionfacts.org/

2. https://www.pcrm.org/

The first one is run by Dr. Greger who is WFPB and does a lot of research on the benefits of WFPB and the harmful effects of animal products and other foods which are not part of a WFPB diet. He has a good search window on his site. For example, here is a link to the effect of dairy products on bone strength: https://nutritionfacts.org/video/is-milk-good-for-our-bones/

Most of his information is in the form of videos in which he presents in the concept in a brief 10-15 minute video.



The second one, PCRM, is also very good. They present a set of general subjects on diet in well done, short essays.
