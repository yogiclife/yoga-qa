**Original Question:**  
And the use of dry roasted Indian spices in satvic food.

**Full Answer:**  
No spice should be "roasted". Roasting involves heating to very high temperatures, which ruins the nutritional qualities of the seed and destroys the oil within it, creating carcinogens.

Bottom line: Food is fuel for our tristariiya existence; it is not bhoga. Our students need to learn to view food in this way and enjoy the whole, natural foods. Going on searching for ways to use food as bhoga, which all the above questions represent, is really not helpful. I know you are already aware of this and practice it in your own life; they also need to learn. The principle is very simple and straightforward.
