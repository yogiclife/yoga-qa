**Full Answer:**  
- Need to make it clear that avocado and nuts are not needed. Some got confused thinking that regular consumption of avocado and nuts are needed for fulfilling the body's need for fat. Hence one person raised the point that these are expensive items for poor people. Whereas the reality is that a WFPB no SOS diet is far less costly than what people eat today.

 

- So be sure not present as though avocados and nuts are the source of fats for a human being. I haven't had a single avocado or a single nut in the last six months. Eating only fruits and vegetables, feeling great. Fruits and vegetables contain all the fat a person needs. A food doesn't need to feel or taste fatty or oily in order to contain fat.
