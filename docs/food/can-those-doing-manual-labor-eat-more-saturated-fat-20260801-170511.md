**Original Question:**  
Clarification by acharya ji.

**Full Answer:**  
- It is a dogma that those who are doing manual labor can eat more saturated fat-containing foods. They will get atherosclerosis, angina, heart attacks, kidney insufficiency and kidney failure, hypertension, stroke just as fast as anyone else. So it is not at all the case that those doing physical labor need more fat in their diet than others. Everyone in the world should eat the same food: WFPB no SOS 80-10-10 diet. The only thing that will vary by person is the quantity of food taken. Naturally, those who are more physically active will need to eat more food-- but the food will be the same.
