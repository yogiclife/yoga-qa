**Full Answer:**  
- Regarding folic acid: It is most abundant in plant foods. 



Foods naturally rich in folate:

Vegetables: Spinach, asparagus, brussels sprouts, collard greens, and other dark green leafy vegetables.

Fruits: Oranges and orange juice, papayas, bananas, strawberries, and grapefruit.

Beans and peas: Lentils, kidney beans, black-eyed peas, and chickpeas.

Nuts: Peanuts, walnuts, and other nuts
