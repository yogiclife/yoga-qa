**Original Question:**  
Does eating good food has a positive effect on the mind as quoted by Mark Hyman ?

**Full Answer:**  
 Eating good food has a very positive effect on the mind.  

Just need to be careful though as to who we quote and highlight. I know this brother well Mark Hyman, he is not a vegetarian. When he says "healthy meals" he is including fish and meat. Here is from Google AI-- "Dr. Mark Hyman recommends a "Pegan" diet, emphasizing vegetables, fruits, nuts, seeds, and healthy fats, along with sustainably raised proteins like fish and pasture-raised meats." Indeed, what he suggests is better than what is provided in juvenile detention centers currently. But what he suggests is itself quite a defective approach, as I know you will agree.

Good food has a very positive effect on the mind. Just need to be careful as t whom we quote.
