**Original Question:**  
clarification on niirjala upavasa, breakfast, avocado,

**Full Answer:**  
A few points regarding their responses this week:



1. The first brother to respond said he did it for two days, then took a break for one day and had his usual breakfast, then did it for another two days, then took a break again for one day. He said he really liked it, but the reason he took a break intermittently was because he was feeling hungry quickly after the raw breakfast, within one hour. 



You can tell him that the reason he felt hungry after one hour is that he did not eat enough food. People are not used to eating the quantity that is needed when one eats raw fruits and vegetables. You replied to him suggesting that he add two bananas as they are more calorie dense, and that is fine but it is also not needed. I have not had any bananas for several months as the farming cooperative I purchase from does not have bananas. As you know, bananas are not locally grown here in this part of the US. Anyhow, my main point is that when people eat raw food, they need to eat far more than they used to eating. When food is cooked, it becomes much more dense. Raw food is not dense. When he eats the raw food breakfast, he needs to eat twice the amount he ate when he tried it last week. Twice the amount. Then he will feel full, and it will last all morning until lunch time. 



2. One sister said she quit tea and coffee for the week and had just raw fruits and vegetables for breakfast. And she really enjoyed it. She did a great job!



3. Someone said they were putting sesame seed oil and salt on their salad for breakfast that week. You correctly replied that the oil and salt are not WFPB food. So they asked about dressings for their salad. There are all sorts of dressings one can use-- on the WFPB and also the raw (80-10-10) websites online there are plenty of recipes for salad dressings. But also, the students can try enjoying the salad just on its own for its own flavor. For myself, I prefer to eat each vegetable separately because each has its own special taste. I never make salads. Although if someone likes making salads there is no problem with that all. 



4. One person said they were eating avocado every day for the raw breakfast. Here I would like to say that avocados and nuts should not be considered staple foods. Both are very high fat. It is fine to consume them sometimes, but not every day. Or if one does consume them every day, then in small amounts. One should not eat a whole avocado daily, nor even half of one. These are special items for occasional consumption.



(One sister said she was eating "avocado toast". Spreading avocado on bread, putting salt on it, and toasting it. This sort of food is eaten purely to satisfy a craving. It has no relation to a raw breakfast. People are so addicted to the wrong sorts of foods. When we guide people what to eat for this experiment we will need to be very clear as to what are the foods that come within the category of raw fruits and vegetables.) 



5. The reason people have so much desire for avocado and nuts is that they still crave the feeling of heaviness which they got with cooked foods. Those cooked foods sit in one's stomach like a huge stone or a big piece of lead. Raw fruits and vegetables don't do that, nor is food supposed to do that. But people like the heavy feeling, and they get that with avocado and nuts. The best thing is to learn to enjoy the light feeling of raw fruits and vegetables. It is wonderful, and that is the spirit of the yogic life. I would not say that someone eating lots of nuts and avocado is following the spirit of the yogic life. The best is to increase the amount of raw fruits and vegetables. People don't eat enough of it. Eating more raw fruits and vegetables is very satisfying, and so good for health-- and most importantly, it is extremely supportive of spiritual growth and going deeper in sadhana.



6. When students asked you what you eat, you said you eat raw fruits and veggies for breakfast, but for lunch and dinner although it is WFPB but "of course" not only raw food. Why "of course"? As you know, raw fruits and vegetables are the best food there is. Try it for one week and see how you like it. You may never go back 😊. 



Overall very nice session! Move ahead Mahodaya!
