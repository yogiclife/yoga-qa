**Original Question:**  
Namaskar Mahodaya, See nowadays lots of people using Herbal Protein powders as supplements. They claim vegetarian diet is not sufficient to fill the protein daily need.  I felt protein powder is required when someone is recovering form illness, but they seem to use it daily. What are the pro's and con's on this.  Please let me know your opinion on the same.  

**Full Answer:**  
Namaskar Bhagini,

Agreed -- you are right. The proportion of macronutrients in plants is perfect for human beings-- 80-10-10. People need not and should not try to alter that proportion by processing the plants in a factory, isolating just one of the macronutrietns, in this case protein, and consuming more of that. Humans have such small minds, they cannot design a better nutritional profile for themselves than what Parama Purusa has created. This is the best: 80-10-10. Excess protein is not only not helpful, it can be harmful.



The above applies to healthy people. As you mentioned, if someone has a chronic illness like metastatic cancer and they are anorexic, losing lots of weight, then as part of their treatment plan this sort of nutritional supplementation can be very helpful. ‎
