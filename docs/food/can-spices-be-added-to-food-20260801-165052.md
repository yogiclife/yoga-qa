**Original Question:**  
Clarification by acharya ji.

**Full Answer:**  
- Tumne kaha ki masala nahiin khana hai. Magar kai masale hain jo bahut acche hain, sattvik hain aur svasthyajanak hain: haldi, jiira, dhaniya, khali mirch. Yah sab bahut accha hai. Pani men hi ubalke baki sab-- chawal, dal, aur agar sabzi pakani hai to vah bhi dal denge. Kya thik nahiin hai? Lahsun, pyaj, namak, tel, lal mirch. Thoda-sa hari mirch bhi thik hi hai, koi dikkat nahiin hai, magar kam hona chahiye-- zyada nahiin.
