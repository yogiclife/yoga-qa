**Original Question:**  
Yoga⁩ Namaskar Aacharya ji. In the last class there was a question regarding fermented food like cooked rice from previous day, idly, dosa batter, curd from soya milk, groundnut milk..etc good in probiotics. Are they satvic?

**Full Answer:**  
Namaskar Bhagini,

Fermented foods are very good. Yes, they are sattvik.
