**Original Question:**  
Clarification by acharya ji.

**Full Answer:**  
- Regarding B12, it should be presented in a very straightforward way. B12 is produced by micro-organisms, and all people are prone to develop low B12 levels irrespective of their diet. Those who eat meat, those who drink milk, those who eat WFPB, all can get deficient in B12 and hence all should supplement it. Finished, end of story. 



You said, "B12 comes from animals and animal products are not supported on a WFPB diet". B12 does not come from animals, it comes from micro-organisms. And there are huge numbers of people in the world who eat meat and dairy and are deficient in B12.



In the last class as well as in this class you said that the source of B12 is the gut of the cow. But B12 is made by micro-organisms throughout the world, in the soil and other places. Not only from the gut of a cow. B12 is not made by cows but rather, as mentioned, by micro-organisms, and those are readily available to humans in lots of vegan sources derived via fungi, yeast etc. So please, forget about the gut of a cow-- if you keep saying this you are going to make students think they need to get their B12 from cows milk. Last week and this week as well you gave this information from your own side, not in response to a question, and it is quite misleading. It is found not only in the gut of a cow but in the gut of pigs, goats, buffalos, chickens etc. Should we start eating all these animals to get the B12 from their gut? I know you don't mean that we should do that, but you are overemphasizing the gut of a cow as well as animals in general as the source of B12. Micro-organisms make B12, not animals. And these micro-organisms are found throughout the world.
