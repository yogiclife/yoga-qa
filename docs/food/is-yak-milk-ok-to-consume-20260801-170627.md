**Full Answer:**  
- One sister asked about use of salt and yak milk in a story she read where one brother had gone to live for a few days with tibetan monks and lived the "yogic life" with them eating only salt water and yak milk. This is nothing but dogma, and I will not consider such a diet to be healthy or "yogika" in any way. It is just dogma. The teaching about WFPB no SOS diet is based on science and stands the same, unaffected by such stories. There is no need to ask for more detail about the life of those at the Tibetan monastery; this diet is not at all healthy and should be neither appreciated, nor followed. It is certainly not a justification for use of salt or dairy. People adopt all sorts of odd practices in the name of religion, which have no rational basis.
