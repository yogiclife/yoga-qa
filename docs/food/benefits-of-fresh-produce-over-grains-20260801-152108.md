**Original Question:**  
Clarification by acharya ji.

**Full Answer:**  
Until the shivir, for the prior 6 months I did not cook anything. I ate only raw fruits and raw vegetables. And that is again what I am doing now.



For years I used to eat 90% raw fruits and vegetables, and just in the evening I would have a cooked grain/bean, along with some fresh raw vegetables. Then, six months ago I stopped preparing the cooked grain/bean in the evening and have been taking only raw fruits and raw vegetables in the evening as well.  It is so beautiful!



Raw fruits and raw vegetables are the key to a long, healthy life and they are the key to having lots of energy and vigor in your daily life.



I have a very large, stainless steel bowl and when I am going to take food I go to the kitchen and fill it with all sorts of raw fruits and vegetables, and just eat them as they are. No knives, forks, or spoons needed! 



Examples of things I fill the bowl with: apples, pears, grapes, bananas, kiwis, oranges, strawberries, blueberries, blackberries, peaches, nectarines, carrots, spinach, bok choy, peas, cauliflower, broccoli, lacinato kale, general kale, tomatoes (several types), sweet pepper (red, green, orange), corn, parsley, cilantro, and several more.



All the above I eat raw: nothing needed! All these items are so tasty, each has its own special flavor and indeed special shakti. Each is a medicine, a source of health, vigor, energy. Each one is alive, and a source of prana. When you eat a live, fresh fruit or vegetable, feel that you are eating prana. The live fruit and live vegetable gives you the prana which it has within it. That is the unique quality of raw, living fruits and vegetables. 



While at the shivir I enjoyed the food there very  much! It is not the food I prefer at home though. We provide cooked foods at the shivir to give everyone a sense of the transitional foods they can use as their taste buds are changing. 



Something important to know: All fresh raw fruits and vegetables are alkaline, and have an alkaline effect on the body. And that is what the body thrives on: it wants an alkaline environment.



In contrast, grains and beans are in general acidic. If you eat a meal of boiled rice only, or boiled rice and dal only, or (wheat) roti only, with no vegetables, you may find that you have an acidic feeling after the meal. That is because these foods are acidic. To me that indicates that the body is not really designed for these foods, as the body wants an alkaline environment whereas they are acidic. Also: if you eat only rice or only roti, you will find that these two foods are constipating. That is another proof that we are not designed to eat them. Yet another proof is that rice and wheat cannot be eaten raw-- they need to be cooked. Same with potatoes-- they have to be cooked. If a food has to be cooked, that is strong evidence that it is not our natural food. Foods that we are designed to eat can be (and should be) eaten raw: nothing need be done to make them edible.



[Follow up] Another proof that we are not designed to eat rice and wheat: people do not like eating them alone. Boiled rice taken alone has no taste. If someone eats just rice or just roti, they do not feel it is tasty - people want to put something on the rice or roti to give it flavor. Nobody likes to eat a meal of only rice. In contrast, fruits and vegetables have a lot of flavor on their own: nothing needs to be added to them. 



Now, if you boil spinach and eat it alone, you may feel it does not have much taste. But if you eat it raw it has a lot of flavor! Same with all the vegetables: each has a wonderful flavor when taken raw. Vegetables are killed and destroyed when boiled and cooked. They should be eaten raw. In contrast, rice and wheat cannot be eaten raw. And when boiled and eaten alone, people feel it has no taste. 



If you feel raw vegetables have no taste, that is only because your taste buds have been dulled by eating all that spicy, salty, oily food. If you eat raw vegetables and no cooked food for one week, your taste buds will again awaken as they revert to their original state, and you will start to love the taste of each and every raw vegetable!
