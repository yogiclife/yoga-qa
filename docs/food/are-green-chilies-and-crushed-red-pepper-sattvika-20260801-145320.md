**Original Question:**  
Mahodaya, I was avoiding green chilies and crushed red pepper, thinking they were rajasic. But since they are not on the rajasic or tamasic list, do we take them as satvic? Thanks.

**Full Answer:**  
Good question Mahodaya. I hadn't mentioned about mirch. Green chilly / hari mirch is by nature sattvika, and red chilly / lal mirch is by nature rajasika. It should be added that this is in small quantity, not in large quanity. If someone puts a huge amount of green chilly in the food due to which the person is perspiring when they eat it, the green chilly will have an agitating effect on the mind and take on a rajasik nature. Similarly, red chilly when taken in large quantity will then have a degenerating effect on the mind and become tamasik. But green chilly, when taken in small quantity so that it has only a very mild effect on the food, is sattvika and can be taken.
