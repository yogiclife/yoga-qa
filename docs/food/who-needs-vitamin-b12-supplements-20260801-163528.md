**Original Question:**  
Clarification by acharya ji.

**Full Answer:**  
- B12: all need to supplement it whether one is vegetarian or not. The only reason it is found to some degree in meat is because at the time of slaughtering an animal, the bacteria in the intestines which produce the B12 in an animal are mixed with the stool, and the stool splatters onto the flesh, contaminating it, and with it comes some B12. But the animal does not have B12 in its flesh; it is only from the bowel movement splattering ont it when it is cut open at the time of slaughter.
