**Original Question:**  
 Why Lord Krsna had so much interest in butter?



**Full Answer:**  
 Cows were treated differently in those days.  Lord Krsna was Himself a great yogii, and He came to this earth to reestablish dharma in human society. He was busy using His precious time for teaching sadhana and spreading manava dharma.
