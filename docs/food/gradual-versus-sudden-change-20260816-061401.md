**Original Question:**  
Gradual versus all-at-once change on food habits

**Full Answer:**  
in response to the bhagini's question about whether to make the shift over to sattvika WFPB all at once or in gradual small steps, your reply was overall very good. I would like to also add something which is an important part of the answer:

- You touched on this first point somewhat, but I want to make it very straight and clear: It is FAR easier to do it all at once. Just as for those who smoke cigarettes and drink alcohol, cutting down gradually is much more difficult and almost impossible to get success with. The easiest way is to cut it out entirely and go through 3-4 days of withdrawal. After that the work is done.

- A critically important corollary to the above is that if one cuts down from one pack of cigarettes per day to a half pack per day, one is not going to feel any better. So one is going through the trauma of depriving oneself of the extra half pack one used to smoke, and one is not getting any reward in return. Whereas if one cuts out cigarettes completely all of a sudden, one will go through withdrawal no doubt for the first three days, but one will at the same time experience a huge improvement in their breathing, and after the first three days they will feel overall much, much better. Same with alcohol. And same with shifting over from poisonous foods to a WFPB diet. A person who makes some tiny changes, cutting down from two cups of milk per day to one cup, decreasing the amount of oil in their food, trying to use a bit less salt, adding a bit of salad to one of the meals-- they are not going to feel any different because they are still eating so much poison. So they are depriving themselves and not experiencing any reward. Instead: Get the poison out of the diet all at once, eliminate ALL of it. REMOVE all SOS. Stop dairy products. Go to the market and buy lots of fruits and veggies and eat them raw. They will feel  SO MUCH BETTER. This is by far the best approach, no comparison.
