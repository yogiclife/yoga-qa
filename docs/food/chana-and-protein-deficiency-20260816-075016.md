**Original Question:**  
Chana and protein deficiency

**Full Answer:**  
- "It is great that you have experimented with moong sprouts. About chick peas, it can be soaked and boiled."



Chana does not need to be boiled. If one soaks them overnight they will sprout, and they are really good. They do not create long sprouts the way moong dal does, over the course of 3-4 days. Rather, just soaking overnight is sufficient, and if one gives them another 24 hours, they develop a small stub which is the beginning of the sprout. That is about as much as they develop, but by eating them raw they are alive and have much more nutritious value than if one boils them.



- Protein deficiency: 

1. The notion that one needs to eat a variety of grains and vegetables in order to get all the essential amino acids was disproven back in the 1950's. There is no need to combine anything. If one were to eat only carrots, or only cabbage, or any single vegetable in a sufficient amount of calories, one would get all the needed amino acids in the needed amounts.

2. The only way to develop protein deficiency is to consume insufficient calories. That is, only those who are starving do not get enough protein. It is called "protein-calorie malnutrition". Those who are starving to death are the only persons who have protein deficiency.

3. If someone is consuming sufficient food to maintain one's weight, one will by definition get enough protein. If one were to eat nothing but potato chips and cookies, in sufficient amount to maintain one's weight, one would get sufficient protein. 

4. So protein is a non-issue so far as deficiency is concerned. There is no need to emphasize a "variety of fruits, grains, beans, vegetables" as it is impossible not to get adequate protein if one is getting adequate calories.

5. As you noted in your message, the concern regarding protein is if one is getting too much-- and that happens when consuming animal products. Not only that, the type of protein one gets from animal products is itself harmful to the human body in various ways.

6. Eating WFPB diet one is guaranteed to get exactly the protein one needs. No need to even think about it.
