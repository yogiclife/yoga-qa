**Original Question:**  
Indian Spices

**Full Answer:**  
 "Indian Spices" cannot be put as a whole in a particular category of food. It depends on the spice. People consider onion and garlic to be spices, and those are tamasika. Red chili is rajasika in small amounts, and tamasika in larger amounts. Green chili is sattvika in small amounts, and rajasika in larger amounts. Most other spices are sattvika-- but one should cook the spices in water and not oil. Most any spice which has not be enlisted in the pdf I created on food, is sattvika.
