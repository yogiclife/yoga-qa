**Full Answer:**  
- The human body needs more potassium than sodium. While adults should consume less than 2,300 mg of sodium per day (official recommendation-- should actually be much less), they need significantly more potassium, with recommendations for adults ranging from 2,600 to 4,700 mg per day. Most people in the US consume too much sodium and not enough potassium, a ratio that can lead to high blood pressure and increase the risk of heart disease and stroke. Potassium is gotten in vegetables.
