**Full Answer:**  
- Coconuts: best amount to eat is zero. Yes, true that it is better to eat fresh local foods. But that doesn't mean that all local foods are good. Onion, garlic, and mushrooms may also be fresh and locally grown-- that doesn't mean we will start eating them. Same way with coconut. The South Indian students want to justify its consumption on the grounds that (1) it is locally grown and (2) it has been traditional food for them for a long time. Neither point #1 nor #2 are justification at all for eating something. Something which is wrong to do cannot be justified on the basis that it has been done for a long time. People have been eating meat for thousands of years: does this fact justify its consumption? No. It has been a mistake from the beginning. Now is the time to correct the mistake. Same with coconut. It is high in saturated fat and is not healthy for human beings.
