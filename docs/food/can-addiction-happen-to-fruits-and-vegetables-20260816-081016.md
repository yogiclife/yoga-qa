**Original Question:**  
 A question regarding intake of fruits and vegetables. When I tell people to eat a lot of fruits and vegetables are there any chances of getting addicted to the fruits? For example, someone falls in love with mangoes or bananas and they can get addicted to that.

**Full Answer:**  
So yes, excellent question, Bhagini. The answer is that there is no chance of addiction to fruits or vegetables as they do not contain addictive ingredients. 



People may enjoy mangoes and bananas, but they will never get addicted to them. Otherwise if there were an opportunity for addiction, the capitalists would have seen that long ago and sought to take advantage of it for making money.



Whenever a person gets addicted to something it is because it contains some substance which is addictive. And that substance can be identified in a laboratory. 



The addictive foods are those containing added salt, oil, and sugar. That is why the capitalists use them to make money. 



Products containing caffeine such as tea and coffee are also quite addictive. We all know people who drink tea and coffee every day. Such people will tell you that they cannot go without it. If they do not get their daily dose of tea/coffee, they will literally get a headache and feel physically bad-- and then when they get their dose of tea/coffee, the symptoms will resolve.  



Dairy cheese is also quite addictive. The addictive substances in cheese are called casomorphins, which are opiate-like compounds produced during the digestion of the milk protein casein. These casomorphins attach to brain receptors, triggering dopamine release and creating a mild, drug-like reward effect. 



Casein is a protein in milk that becomes highly concentrated when making cheese. When consumed, casein breaks down into casomorphins, which act as weak opiates, sometimes referred to as "dairy crack" due to their addictive properties. These compounds can trigger the same dopamine receptors in the brain as addictive drugs, leading to cravings.



Bananas and mangoes are tasty no doubt, beautifully so-- but they are not addictive. You will not find a single person on the planet who cannot go a day without a banana or mango. Nor is there anyone who goes into withdrawal and gets headaches or tremors if they do not get their "banana fix" or their "mango fix". It will never happen-- these foods do not contain any addictive substances to produce such effects.
