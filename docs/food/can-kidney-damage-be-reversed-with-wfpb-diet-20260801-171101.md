**Original Question:**  
Namaskar Mahodaya, conducted X bhagini's class yesterday and there was a question yesterday from one of them.



Q) Bhagini says one of her close relative who is 40yrs old is suffering from CKD ( chronic Kidney disease ) and is on dialysis ? Would WFPB diet reverse the disease ?

Told her that we cannot stop  the Kidney damage but the progress of the disease might be slowed down. But in all cases said I would check with my acharya and get back. So wanted to confirm if this answer is accurate or not ? Please let me know...

**Full Answer:**  
In most cases when the kidneys have become so badly damaged that the person needs dialysis then the damage cannot be reversed, but there are some rare cases where a person may be able to reverse the kidney damage by going to WFPB no SOS. It depends on the degree of damage as well as the cause of CKD. But in any case, whether or not the person's kidneys can be saved, they will benefit tremendously from WFPB no SOS diet. So the dietary change should be done to see what benefits they can get.
