**Original Question:**  
For the absorption of fat soluble vitamins does the body need some fat?

**Full Answer:**  
Yes, the body does need some fat, it is an essential nutrient that one needs to ingest from food-- for absorption of fat soluble vitamins as well as for many other bodily functions such as the production of hormones. But it is important to know that every fruit, vegetable, grain, and bean contains fat. And indeed, these whole foods contain all the fat one needs. As we have discussed at the shivir, all whole plant foods are around 80% carbohydrate, 10% protein, and 10% fat. And the fat they contain is the very best there is, exactly the type one needs. Almost completely mono-unsaturated and poly-unsaturated fats. So there is no question of needing to "supplement" by adding fat to the diet i.e. as vegetable oil or ghee, etc. These are all very harmful items and should be avoided completely; there is no need for them.
