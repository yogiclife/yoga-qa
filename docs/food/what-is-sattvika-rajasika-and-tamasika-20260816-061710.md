**Original Question:**  
What is sattvika, rajasika, and tamasika

**Full Answer:**  
Regarding categorization of foods as sattvika, rajasika, and tamasika, those are classifications which were done ages ago in ancient times, and one cannot easily change them. So vegetable oil is sattvika, foods deep fried in boiling vegetable are sattvika, samosa is sattvika, all the dairy products are sattvika, and honey is sattvika. I do not consider any of these foods sattvika and I do not consume them. Perhaps in the future there will be scope to remove these foods from the sattvika category. It gets a bit old to have to keep repeating, "officially it is sattvika but...". We should just remake the chart ourselves.
