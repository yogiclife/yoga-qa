**Original Question:**  
Clarification by acharya ji.

**Full Answer:**  
You had a long back-and-forth with one sister whose name is not in my mind now (I listened to the recording several days ago -- perhaps X ji?). She was resistant to the idea that humans are more developed than animals, pointing out that humans build boundaries between humans, whereas animals do not do this between animals. You tried explaining to her a number of times, but she was not ready to accept the various things you said.



The answer is straight-forward, and if she is sincere she will readily accept it: Humans have a far more developed mind than animals (this much you said). And along with that developed mind, they have the faculty of free will (this much also you said). Now, putting these two concepts together: In this universe, humans are have the highest developed mind, and along with that they have the freedom to properly use that faculty, or they can misuse it. This option to use or to misuse is not something animals have. Their mind is far less developed, but what mind they have they are only able to properly use; they do not have the freedom to misuse it. 



In contrast, humans have the freedom to use or to misuse their minds. If they properly use their mental faculty, they will become divine in human form and merge into the Supreme Entity. If they misuse their mental faculty, they will degenerate themselves and become lower than animals-- and accordingly they will get reborn as stones and as insects. It is part of their developed intellect that they have the freedom to use it properly or to misuse it. Seema ji was simply giving example of the case in which humans misuse their intellect. But that does not indicate that humans are less developed than animals; rather, it is simply illustrating that humans have the freedom to misuse the developed intellect which they possess. Animals cannot even understand about building boundaries-- such a concept is beyond their mental capacity. Animals can neither degenerate themselves, nor can they consciously elevate themselves. They can only behave according to their inborn instincts, and in so doing, through struggle to survive they evolve at a fixed rate of speed. Whereas humans can degenerate themselves or they can consciously elevate themselves, that to quite rapidly, to reach the pinnacle of development which is merger in Brahma, the Supreme Entity.
