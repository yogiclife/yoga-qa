**Original Question:**  
With regard to the infinite desires, I realize that all humans have a desire for the infinite. I was trying to contrast lazy people, who have no ambitions, versus ambitious people who put in a lot of effort in material success. It seems to me that the former are tamasika. Even if they they got convinced by spirituality, they would never put in the effort to succeed. If the ambitious rajasika persons got convinced of spirituality on the other hand, we can expect them to go full force into spirituality. So, I was thinking that the rajasika persons are better than the tamasika ones.

**Full Answer:**  
 Your point about lazy versus ambitious people is well taken. Laziness is definitely a tamasik quality. And of those who are materialistically ambitious, yes there is a dominance of rajas in them-- but most of them are rajasik with a strong tamasik tint to it such that their lifestyles are very negative and they have no interest whatsoever in sattvika things nor can they be convinced. And many of these are so selfish that they do not hesitate to exploit others. Abusing their infinite desire for acquisition of huge amounts of finite things is a very crude and tamasik tendency which degrades them in the worst possible way. So these are not people dominated by rajas really but by tamas, and they get reborn as stones in their next life. They are far worse than animals. Pashoh adhamah. There is a very small subset of such materialistically ambitious people who experience some big event in their life -- either a physical or psychic event -- which changes their lives and opens their mind to redirection toward the Infinite Entity. And for them, yes-- they can become very good indeed.
