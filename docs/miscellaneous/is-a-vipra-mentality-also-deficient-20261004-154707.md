**Original Question:**  
Thanks, mahodaya. I also want to let my class know about the difference between the different sevas and the different mentalities. In case there is a question about it, is the vipra mentality -- which I guess we will study later -- also deficient, being bound by gunas? So, only a yogic mentality would be right?

**Full Answer:**  
Yes Mahodaya, you are correct. Most of the vipras are degenerated, bound by dogmas. A sadhaka, having what you have called the yogic mentality, is above the four varnas.
