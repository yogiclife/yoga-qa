**Original Question:**  
"If we say all the rituals and religious practices useless, what should a person engage in to move ahead towards 12 O’clock?" This person has not yet learnt Sadhana and practicing few yogic life practices. She is not able to come for shivir due to those usual excuse of family gatherings. How can she be helped?

**Full Answer:**  


As you know, we are not merely saying this-- it is true. All the rituals and religious practices are useless. And not only useless, they bring about a person's degeneration and make it more difficult for them to come onto the path of dharma.



"This person has not yet learnt Sadhana and practicing few yogic life practices. She is not able to come for shivir due to those usual excuses of family gatherings. How can she be helped?"



You can bring a horse to water but you can't make it drink. The sense of this question is, "How can we help a person to follow dharma who themselves does not want to follow dharma?" If this sister wanted to learn sadhana, she would attend the yoga shivir. If she were ready to follow dharma, she would do whatever she needed to attend the shivir. But she is giving the same excuse that everyone else gives. It means she is not ready. There are always obstacles. One has to become ready to traverse them-- and she is clearly saying she is not ready to do that. 



If you want to help her, the way to do it is to encourage her and help her prepare her mind to overcome the obstacles involved in attending the yoga shivir. Those obstacles will always be there. Does she think they won't be there next year? They will be there next year and every year after that. So shubhasya shiighram. She needs to take drdha samkalpa and attend this very year. She can do it! This is the way to help her. She is surrounded by dogma in her life-- the one way to break through and overcome it is to attend the yoga shivir and ensconce herself in a dharmic environment.
