**Original Question:**  
he brother who was challenging whether humans really have free will or not because, as he was saying, everything is predestined anyway i.e. the end result is already decided by the Supreme Entity and the chain of karya-karana-bhava needed to achieve that end is also decided by Him, and as a result no one actually has free will at all. 

**Full Answer:**  
This idea is totally bogus, and his presentation is purely hypocritical: he does not feel like this at all in his life, he is just putting forth this idea with the desire to challenge what you are saying. He does not himself believe what he is saying. The proof is that he makes hundreds of decisions a day in his own life, and as he makes them he does not at all feel as though the decisions are already decided. Does he undergo stress as he makes the various decisions? Surely-- and the reason is that he feels pressure to make the right choice. So he feels like he is the one making the choice.



 And this is the case with all human beings. People feel like they have freedom to choose what they think is best at every moment, and that is because they do. That Parama Purusa knows everything of the past, present, and future and therefore knows what is going to happen and what someone is going to choose, does not at all mean that a person does not have freedom to choose. 



Those are two entirely different matters: on the one hand humans freedom to choose, and on the other hand Parama Purusa's sarvajinata. Both coexist, and neither is in opposition to the other. They both exist and one cannot argue with sincerity against this fact. That he tried to do so is proof of his lack of sincerity. Humans have free will from their perspective and it is a real perspective. And PP is all-knowing and that is real. But WE are not all-knowing. 



Only the person who is all-knowing has the right to say there is no free will and everything is pre-destined. Your student is not all-knowing and as a result, does not at all feel in his heart that everything he does is fixed and pre-destined. If he really felt that way, then he would not have an ounce of stress in his life, which is not at all the case. The person who claims to feel that he has no freedom to choose in his life is just lying through his teeth; no one feels like that and the reason they don't feel like that is because it is not the case.
