**Original Question:**  
Mahodaya, one student wanted to know the Sanskrit word for condensed as used in explaining why prakriti is able to qualify brahma that is less condensed. Is the use of condensed in this context a technical term used in yoga darshana with a Sanskrit equivalent?



**Full Answer:**  
The word for condensed in this setting in Sanskrit is घन.
