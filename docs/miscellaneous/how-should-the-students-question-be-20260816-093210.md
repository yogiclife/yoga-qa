**Original Question:**  
How should the students question be ?

**Full Answer:**  
Students questions should be like playing devil's advocate, just trying to challenge things  As we say, questions should be pariprashna i.e. questions asked with a view toward implementing the teaching in one's life, and that they never do. Even the next layer, i.e. questions asked with a sincere desire to learn.
