**Original Question:**  
Is the correct pronunciation "स्याम्‌" or "स्याम"

**Full Answer:**  
Very small point = एकोऽहं बहु स्याम्‌ । In the last word, स्याम् is a halantashabda so the pronunciation ends with 'm', not 'ma'. That is, it is pronounced स्याम्‌, not "स्या-मअ" ।
