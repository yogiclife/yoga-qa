**Original Question:**  
Mahodaya, I recall that you had once mentioned that lord Shiva introduced the idea of marriage. Since kshatriya society started it, did lord Shiva appear during the kshatriya era?

**Full Answer:**  
The name of the father of Lord Shiva is not known, because at that time there was no marriage system. That was the Shudra Age. Lord Shiva's teachings ushered in the transition to the Ksatriya Age.
