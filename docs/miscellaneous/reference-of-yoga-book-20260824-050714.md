**Original Question:**  
One student wanted references to read more about yoga. Do you have any suggestions?



**Full Answer:**  
Regarding the student's question about references at the end of class, your answer was perfect. He will be getting everything he needs in your class, and there is no book in which he will receive any of this. And he is already getting more than he can manage. It is not as though he is devouring everything you gave him and is in need of more. Has he incorporated the changes in his life which you have guided him to do? It is very good that he did nirjalopavasa a few times. Is he doing it regularly twice a month? And what about food-- I doubt he has made much change in his food, not in accordance with our standards. And vyapaka shaoca? And daily exercise? If he is not doing these things, he has not learned hardly anything. He is still residing in the dogmatic Hindu world which regards "learning" as a strictly mental exercise. That is not learning. If he wants resources, point him to the resource of eating a WFPB diet. Resources come in many forms, and he is not utilizing hardly any of the resources you have provided him. You can guide and encourage him to chew, digest, and incorporate the material we have given him, and then we will give him more.
