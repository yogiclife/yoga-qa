**Original Question:**  
Mahodaya, what is the difference between satyam used in the sense of absolute reality and सत्, for example when we characterize brahma (saguna?) as sat-chit-ananda?



**Full Answer:**  
The grammatical derivation of satyam is given as: सत्यं,  क्ली (सते हितम् । सत् + यत् ।) 

- In a Philosophical setting they are conveying the same meaning: absolute truth
