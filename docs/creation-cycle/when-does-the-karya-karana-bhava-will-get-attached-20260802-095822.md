**Original Question:**  
When the karya karana bhava will get attached ?

**Full Answer:**  
This refers to causality. Causal relationships are a part of srishti and occur just after srishti. This is not related to jivas. For example, a stone will fall due to gravity. Gravity is the karana and falling is the karya. Karma phala is one instance of the result of causality, but there are others, like a stone falling, that are the result of causality but not karma phala.
