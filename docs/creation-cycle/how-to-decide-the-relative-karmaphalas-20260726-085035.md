**Original Question:**  
Will X yield worse karmaphala or Y'. I am not sure I can evaluate the relative karmaphalas, and I am not sure we need answers for those either. For example, there was a question on whether robbing a rich man will yield less sorrow than robbing a poor man. I thought I would answer such questions along the lines that the knowledge of karmaphala is meant to guide us take the right action. We should rob neither the rich nor the poor. That way, we don't get any karmaphala. (Of course, we needed something to survive, then taking it from the rich would be preferable to taking from the poor, I guess.) Basically, if the answer to a question does not the students  in some manner, then I don't need to find the answer. Would this approach work?



**Full Answer:**  
Effect of a particular karmaphala depends on a particular individual, the other samskaras they have, their circumstances in life, the level of elevation of their mind before they experienced that karmaphala. There are many factors, and so we really cannot evaluate what effect the karmaphala will have on person's mind. After all, that is really what matters, right? And not the superficial material effect it may have i.e. gain or loss of money, physical harm or benefit etc.
