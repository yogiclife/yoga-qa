**Original Question:**  
I am trying to relate what is happening in saguna brahma's chitta with respect to srishti with our observations of the natural world. At the level of saguna brahma's chitta, we can see that some part of the chitta takes the shape of a stone, due to qualities imposed by prakriti, and we see it as a stone in the natural world. Over the srishti chakra, the part of saguna brahma's chitta that was qualified like a stone becomes subtler and may eventually become a component of the mind. This would lead to the disappearance of this stone for an observer in the natural world. Is this what is happening? We don't see stones disappearing like that because the time scales involved for such transition are very large, is it?

**Full Answer:**  
- It is difficult to comment on the time scale here; it may indeed be that the time scale is large, it is also likely that the size scale is extremely low, microscopic, so that one would never see a stone disappear even when the transition was happening.

- Also, it may be that this transition is most marked in locations in the srsti where life has not yet emerged. And that in places like the earth where life is well established, the ksititattva is no longer converting to life forms.

- This would be a subject for researchers to explore. They should be sadhakas as well as scientists, in order to be able to think deeply and in the proper direction. Materialist scientists will not be able to engage in the proper endeavors so as to arrive at the answers to these questions.
