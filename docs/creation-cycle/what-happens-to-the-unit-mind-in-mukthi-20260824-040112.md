**Original Question:**  
More on Mukti

**Full Answer:**  
In explaining about what mukti is you said that in mukti the unit mind no longer has citta or ahamtattva, and only its mahattattva remains so there is the I-feeling. But actually what happens is that in mukti the unit mind merges into Cosmic Mind, and as such no components of unit mind remain. The unit mind's "I-feeling" also does not remain. The analogy of the drop of water and the ocean works here as well. The unit mind is the drop of water, and it merges into Cosmic Mind which is the ocean. Once it merges, all that remains is Cosmic Mind. The unit mind is there, but it no longer has its identity as unit mind; it has become wholly merged in Cosmic Mind. So the unit mind's "I-feeling" is no longer there; there is "I-feeling", but that is the "I-feeling"  of Cosmic Mind and so what was the unit mind now identifies as the "I-feeling" of Cosmic Mind only.
