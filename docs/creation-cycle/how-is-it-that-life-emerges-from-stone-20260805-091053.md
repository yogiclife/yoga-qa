And to give the fullest, most complete reply is to remind them as we discussed above in point 2 with the butter-milk contradiction, that ksititattva or stone is not inert at all (rather than saying that it is truly inert) and is not in reality crude, as it is purely the thought-projection of Cosmic Mind. So being alive (albeit not yet outwardly evident), for life to emerge from it in congenial conditions is quite a natural and intuitive process.
