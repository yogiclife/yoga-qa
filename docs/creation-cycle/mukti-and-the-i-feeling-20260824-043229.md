**Original Question:**  
 can you clarify your following prior explanation on mukti to Anita ji? _'So the feeling of "I" with which we identify is held back at the level of Cosmic Mind, as a result of which when the jiivatma merges into Nirguna Purusa the unit feeling of "I" doesn't go with it.'_  I thought that holding back the feeling of "I" was the feeling of unit "I". Am I right in understanding from the recent explanation that the unit mind no longer exists as a separate entity and so could not retain the unit 'I' feeling? So, the cosmic mind is what retains its feeling of a cosmic 'I'. In case of moksha, that piece of unit mind would have reached a nirguna state and no longer be a part of the cosmic mind.



**Full Answer:**  
The sentence which you have cited here is meant to indicate that because in mukti the unit mind merges into Cosmic Mind, so it (unit mind) is unable to merge into Nirguna Purusa. Unit mind can only merge into one thing; once it merges, it has merged and cannot again regain its unit-ness so as to later merge into something else. "Held back" does not mean that it retains its identity but that it merges into Cosmic Mind and thus cannot move ahead to merge into Nirguna Purusa.
