**Original Question:**  
With respect to the third point, yes, I did mean that the rebirth could be in a lower body based on the previous human samskaras. 

**Full Answer:**  
With respect to the third point, it is important to understand that the forward or backward movement of a particular mind when it gets a new birth has nothing to do with what it "did" as a bodiless mind. That mind was in transit and just awaiting placement in a new body. If we are to say that it can "devolve" then we need to say that it can "evolve" as well. But in reality it does neither; whatever body it gets in its next birth is determined solely on what it did in its previous life when it had a body.
