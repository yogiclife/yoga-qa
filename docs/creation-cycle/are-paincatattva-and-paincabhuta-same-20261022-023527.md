**Original Question:**  
Tattva - is the fundamental principle( Kshiti tatvaa - The earth principle)  and Panchabhutas - are core gross elements. Can it be stated this way mahodaya ?

**Full Answer:**  
Paincatattva and Paincabhuta are the same thing. In English it is the five fundamental factors.
