**Original Question:**  
 For example during evolution we take dog as example , some dog is living sophisticated life from birth in rich man house, at the same time some are even struggling for one time food"

**Full Answer:**  
The normal process is for all beings to struggle on the path of spiritual evolutions. Just like we have to perform exercise to strengthen our muscles, our mind needs to be strengthened through struggle. But they may go through different struggles. Like different exercises can strengthen different muscles. There is one more point. Although an animal that has never experience human birth has no karma phala to expiate, some animals have led human birth in the past and fallen. They may experience joy or sorrow in excess of the usual struggle of evolution.
