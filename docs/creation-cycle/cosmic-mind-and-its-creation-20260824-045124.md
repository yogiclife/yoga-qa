**Original Question:**  
Mukti, merger of unit mind into cosmic mahattattva?

**Full Answer:**  
Mukti. The correction you provided at the beginning of the session was proper. Just I am providing an addendum here to it, want to make sure this is also understood. While it is indeed the case that when unit mind gets mukti it merges into the mahattattva of Cosmic Mind, it is also the case that the Cosmic Mind's mahattattva is not something static that always remains as mahattattva. As we know, sattvaguna is always qualifying nirguna purusa to create more Cosmic mahattattva, and rajoguna is working continuously qualifying the mahattattva of Cosmic Mind to create more Cosmic ahamtattva, and tamoguna is in turn working continuously qualifying Cosmic ahamtattva to create more Cosmic citta, and that very tamoguna is also working continuously qualifying Cosmic citta to create more paincatattva---akashatattva to ksititattva. All of this is an ongoing process. And so naturally when unit mind merges into Cosmic mahattattva, it becomes part of the reservoir from which Cosmic ahamtattva is being made. So while the unit mind that gets mukti does not get reborn as that same unit mind, as mind-stuff it does get recycled into the srsti just as occurs with the entire Cosmic Mind. Similar to how water flows from the river to the ocean, then evaporates from the ocean to become the clouds in the sky, then becomes the rain that rains on the mountains and again supplies the river with water, the cycle goes on and on.
