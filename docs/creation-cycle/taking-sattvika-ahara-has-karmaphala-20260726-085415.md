**Original Question:**  
You had mentioned that karmaphala is meant to take the mind from a distorted state to neutral state. For a human, is the neutral state one in which we meditate on paramapurusha and work toward serving him? So, sadhana, seva, asanas, etc would not yield karmaphala. Anything we do as ordained by paramapurusha is not independent action, and is thus not subject to karmaphala. Eating sattvika ahara would also not yield karmaphala, because even though we are dealing with the finite, we are doing it because paramapurusha wants us to eat that to maintain a healthy body. Is this right?



**Full Answer:**  
- So, sadhana, seva, asanas, etc would not yield karmaphala. Anything we do as ordained by paramapurusha is not independent action, and is thus not subject to karmaphala. Eating sattvika ahara would also not yield karmaphala, because even though we are dealing with the finite, we are doing it because paramapurusha wants us to eat that to maintain a healthy body. Is this right?



Each of these actions depends on the motivation with which they are done. If someone takes sattvika food with the ideation that they are feeding PP then it is one thing; if they take sattvika food with greediness and are intoxicated with the taste of the food and overeat, then it is another thing. Every action depends on the motivation with which it is done. But yes, if the activities of yoganistha jiivanam are engaged in with proper ideation, then there will be no samskara created. And while doing sadhana, samskaras are burned i.e. finished.
