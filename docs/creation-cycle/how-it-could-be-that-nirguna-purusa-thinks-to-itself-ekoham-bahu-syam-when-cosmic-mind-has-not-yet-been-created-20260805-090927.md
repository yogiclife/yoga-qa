**Original Question:**  
How it could be that Nirguna Purusa thinks to itself "ekoham bahu syam" when Cosmic Mind has not yet been created?

**Full Answer:**  
X ji is asking how it could be that Nirguna Purusa thinks to itself "ekoham bahu syam" when Cosmic Mind has not yet been created. In reply, you explained that this question is beyond karyakarana bhava. X ji was not satisfied and again asked, "How could NP have even thought this, if it cannot even think "I am" and therefore required the creation of Cosmic Mind?" Simply saying the question is beyond KKB is not and was not satisfactory.



Although it is true that NP is beyond karyakarana bhava, that is not really the proper reply here, as being beyond karyakarana bhava does not give us license to start creating all sorts of unreasonable explanations as to how the creation begins. The explanation must seem reasonable.  And as evidence of the unreasonableness of this explanation, X ji was not satisfied and asked her follow-up question which was quite justified. If NP has no mind and cannot think anything, then how could it possibly have the bhava "ekoham bahu syam"?



So here the proper reply is that Shuddha Purusa, Nirguna Purusa, is not without intelligence-- it is not without mind the way that a stone is without mind and therefore cannot think. Rather, Shuddha Purusa i.e. Nirguna Purusa is even higher than Mind. It is "super-mind" i.e. above mind, it is super-consciousness. It is not that Nirguna Purusa does not know anything-- rather it knows everything, it is sarvajina. Everything is within it. Nothing is outside of it. True, it does not think in a formal sense, but it does not need to. It is even higher than mind, and as such can easily have the bhava "ekoham bahu syam". Purusa is above, and more powerful even in intelligence, than Mind-- and not at all utterly incapable as stone. The situation of stone being without its own mind, and Shuddha Purusa being without Its own Mind, cannot be compared.



The point of being "beyond karya-karana-bhava" is really the answer to a different question. If the reason for srsti is explained as NP's bhava "ekoham bahu syam" and in reply someone asks, "But Brahma is complete unto Itself, there is no abhava, nothing needed which It does not have. Everything is within It. So when it is already complete unto Itself, why would Brahma think 'ekoham bahu syam'?" In response to this question only, the answer is that this matter is "beyond karya-karana-bhava". In other words, this is the reply when the question is as to WHY the srsti was created, what was the need for it.



But if someone asks HOW shuddha purusa could think "ekoham bahu syam" when mind is not even created yet and NP cannot even think "I am", then in response one needs to explain about the inate, intuitive super-consciousness of NP. That is the reply. If one instead says only that it is "beyond karya-karana-bhava", this will not be satisfactory, and other questions regarding the reply inevitably arise.
