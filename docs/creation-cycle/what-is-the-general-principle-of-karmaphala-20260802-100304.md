**Original Question:**  
What is the general principle of karmaphala ?

**Full Answer:**  
A general principle that where there is samskara there will be karmaphala. Where there is no samskara there will be no karmaphala. Animals are not the doers of their actions hence they do not create samskara by the actions they engage in, and hence there is no karmaphala for those actions. If an insect flies into a fire or an animal falls out of a tree or off a rooftop, that is part of their samgharsha and samiti process and they will be growing by the struggles they have to face, even if it results in their death.
