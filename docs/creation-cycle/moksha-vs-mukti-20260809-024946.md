**Original Question:**  
What happens in Moksha and in Mukti?

**Full Answer:**  
So in moksa the unit mind dissolves or merges into jiivatma which then merges into Nirguna Purusa. In mukti the unit mind merges into Cosmic Mind. Jiivatma merges into Nirguna Purusa here as well, but the difference is that unit mind has not merged into jiivatma. So the feeling of "I" with which we identify is held back at the level of Cosmic Mind, as a result of which when the jiivatma merges into Nirguna Purusa the unit feeling of "I" doesn't go with it.
