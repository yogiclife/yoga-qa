**Original Question:**  
whether something which is reflected can be the source of the thing reflecting it,

**Full Answer:**  
This question shows a fundamental lack of understanding about srsticakra. The term "reflection" only comes into play in pratisaincara. There is no process of "reflection" going on in saincara, wherein mind is created. The process of reflection begins only after 6:00, wherein the mahatattva has a reflection of Paramatma within it as jiivatma. And this reflection is quite vague in the beginning, and becomes stronger as the jiiva evolves and ultimately becomes a human being. Sanjiv Mahodaya needs to understand srsticakra an d then his profound confusion regarding "reflection" will cease to exist.
