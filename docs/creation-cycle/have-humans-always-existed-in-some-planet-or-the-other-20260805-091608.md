**Original Question:**  
Have humans always existed in some planet or the other? The reason for this question is that if humans exist only on earth, then most of the time in the srishti process would have been uninteresting, with no one having free will to perform sadhana, in fact, with no life. If other planets with life existed, then it seems a more elegant model with some unit consciousnesses always attaining moksha while others are entering srishti. It would be some type of dynamic steady equilibrium.



**Full Answer:**  
There is life in various locations throughout the universe, and that includes human life. I cannot say when human life began, but it is there and has been there in locations other than this planet. The history of life in the universe is an ancient one indeed.
