The srishti chakra suggests that it started at 3 o'clock. So, the process has a definite beginning but there is no ending. Is this correct?

we cannot say that we are at year X from the beginning of creation. So, time goes to negative infinity in the past and positive infinity in the future. Is this right?





**Full Answer:**  
Here the point is that there are countless beginnings of the srsti at countless locations within the ocean of Nirguna Purusa. And these beginnings have been happening without any identifiable "adi". In the future also, in places within Nirguna Purusa where there never was any srsti, it may begin there also-- and that will be a new beginning in that location. So yes there are countless beginnings of the srsti. Theoretically we can speak of an adi for the srsti, but practically that adi has been going on since time immemorial.

I appreciate your deep interest and inquisitive mind regarding the various aspects of this process. Regarding the transition from ksititattva to the manifestation of life:

- It is difficult to comment on the time scale here; it may indeed be that the time scale is large, it is also likely that the size scale is extremely low, microscopic, so that one would never see a stone disappear even when the transition was happening.

- Also, it may be that this transition is most marked in locations in the srsti where life has not yet emerged. And that in places like the earth where life is well established, the ksititattva is no longer converting to life forms.

- This would be a subject for researchers to explore. They should be sadhakas as well as scientists, in order to be able to think deeply and in the proper direction. Materialist scientists will not be able to engage in the proper endeavors so as to arrive at the answers to these questions.
